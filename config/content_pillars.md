# Content Pillars
> These are the six things I write about. Every LinkedIn post should map to one primary pillar.
> Edit this file to add angles, retire pillars, or shift emphasis.
> The post-generator uses this to frame every post's perspective and CTA.

---

## Pillar 1 — AI Tool Experiments (Human in the Loop)

**What it is:** Personal accounts of testing AI tools in real brand and marketing contexts. I do the experiment, then report back honestly — what worked, what didn't, what surprised me, what I'd do differently.

**Why it matters:** Most LinkedIn AI content is either press releases or pure hype. First-hand experiments with specific results cut through. This is my most distinctive pillar.

**Angle starters:**
- "I used [tool] to [specific task] for [duration/project]. Here's what actually happened."
- "I ran [AI tool] against [real brief]. The gap between what I expected and what I got was revealing."
- "I've been experimenting with [feature] for 2 weeks. Here's the honest version."
- "The workflow I actually use: [step by step breakdown using AI tools]"

**Key brands/tools I test:**
- Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini (for research, writing, strategy)
- Midjourney, Runway, Sora (for visual and video creative)
- ElevenLabs (for voice and audio branding)
- Canva AI, Adobe Firefly (for design)
- Claude Code, Cursor (for no-code/low-code builds)

**Trigger to write this post:** When I complete a meaningful experiment with an AI tool on a real brief or project.

## Pillar 2 — New AI Launches & Feature Drops

**What it is:** Fast-turn reaction posts when a major AI company ships something new — a model, a feature, an integration, a product demo, or a major YouTube announcement.

**Why it matters:** Being first with a clear, opinionated take on a launch builds credibility as someone who watches this space closely.

**Companies I track closely:**
- OpenAI (ChatGPT, GPT series, Sora, Operator)
- Anthropic (Claude 3/4 series, Claude Code, MCP)
- Google DeepMind (Gemini, NotebookLM, Veo, Imagen, Project Astra)
- Perplexity
- ElevenLabs
- Midjourney
- xAI / Grok
- Meta AI / LLaMA series
- Mistral / Le Chat
- Runway / Pika (video generation)

**Angle starters:**
- "[Company] just launched [feature]. Here's why it matters for brands — not just developers."
- "The thing everyone missed in [Company]'s announcement."
- "[New model] is out. I've had a few hours with it. First impressions."

**Trigger to write this post:** A major product launch, model release, or feature update from a tracked company.

## Pillar 3 — AI × Marketing & Brand Strategy

**What it is:** How brands and marketing teams can practically apply AI — in campaigns, creative workflows, strategy, research, content, and measurement.

**Why it matters:** This is where I add the most unique value. I bridge AI capability (what it can do) with brand application (why a CMO or brand team should care).

**Angle starters:**
- "What [AI development] means for brand teams."
- "The brief just changed. Here's why AI makes [specific creative task] different now."
- "Three ways brands are using [AI tool/feature] in campaigns right now."
- "If I were briefing my creative team today, [AI development] would change these two things."

**Topics in this pillar:**
- AI in campaign concepting and creative briefing
- AI for brand research and audience insight
- AI in content production workflows
- Personalisation at scale
- AI in social media strategy
- AI and creative brand identity (logos, visual systems)
- The future of brand voice when AI can mimic anyone

**Trigger to write this post:** When an AI development has a direct, non-obvious implication for brand or marketing teams.

## Pillar 4 — AI Startup Funding & Ecosystem

**What it is:** When a notable AI startup closes a significant funding round, is acquired, launches a product, or hits a valuation milestone — and what it signals about where the market is heading.

**Why it matters:** Funding news tells you what smart money thinks is worth building. It's a leading indicator for where AI is heading.

**Angle starters:**
- "[Startup] raised $Xm. Here's what that tells us about where AI is actually going."
- "Three AI companies got funded this week. One story didn't get the attention it deserved."
- "The [Series X] round for [company] is bigger than the headline number."

**What I look for in a funding story:**
- Unusually large or early-stage amounts
- Categories that signal emerging trends (agent frameworks, voice AI, vertical AI)
- Investors or founders with notable track records
- Acquisitions by big tech that fly under the radar

**Trigger to write this post:** A funding round, acquisition, or major valuation that signals a category shift.

## Pillar 5 — Big Tech AI Strategy

**What it is:** When Microsoft, Apple, Google, Amazon, Meta, or Nvidia make a significant AI-related move — a new product, a partnership, a hire, a policy position, or a strategic bet.

**Why it matters:** Big tech AI decisions have downstream effects on every product, platform, and marketing channel brands rely on.

**Companies I watch:**
- Microsoft (Copilot integration into Microsoft 365, Azure AI)
- Apple (Apple Intelligence, on-device AI)
- Google (AI in Search, Gemini in Workspace)
- Meta (AI features in social platforms, LLaMA releases)
- Amazon (AWS AI, Alexa+, Bedrock)
- Nvidia (chips, infrastructure, NIM platform)
- Salesforce (Agentforce, Einstein AI)
- Adobe (Firefly, GenFill)

**Angle starters:**
- "The [Company] announcement that CMOs need to understand."
- "[Big Tech move] signals something much bigger for how [channel/platform] works."
- "What [Company]'s AI bet actually means for the brands who depend on their platforms."

**Trigger to write this post:** A platform or infrastructure change that affects how brands use the channel.

## Pillar 6 — Opinion & Hot Takes

**What it is:** Standalone opinion posts on AI trends, narratives, or debates — where I take a clear, sometimes contrarian position.

**Why it matters:** Opinions generate conversation. These posts often get the most comments and shares because people want to agree, disagree, or add nuance.

**Format:** These can be shorter (300–500 chars). Sometimes a single provocation is enough.

**Angle starters:**
- "The [mainstream AI take] is wrong. Here's why."
- "Everyone is talking about [X]. Nobody is talking about [Y]."
- "My honest take on [AI trend] after [timeframe] of using it."
- "The hype cycle for [AI thing] is real. So is the legitimate case for it."

**Topics I have opinions on:**
- AI replacing creative jobs (nuanced take: it replaces tasks, not roles — but that's not neutral)
- AI and brand authenticity (can a brand sound human if AI wrote it?)
- The race to build AI agents (most aren't ready for real work yet)
- Prompt engineering as a skill (overrated vs. underrated debate)
- AI in education and what it means for how we learn brand skills

**Trigger to write this post:** When I have a strong opinion I want tested in public.
