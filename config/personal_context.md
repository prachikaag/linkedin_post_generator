## My Current Opinions on AI + Brands

<!-- These are your takes — add to them as your thinking evolves. -->
<!-- The post-generator uses these to make "MY TAKE" sections sound like you. -->

1. **Most brands are still treating AI as a productivity shortcut, not a strategic shift.** The companies that get ahead will be the ones that use it to think differently, not just work faster.

2. **The "AI will replace creatives" narrative is wrong.** What's actually happening: AI raises the floor for mediocre work while raising the ceiling for people who know how to direct it.

3. **The speed of AI development is genuinely hard to keep up with** — and that itself is the competitive advantage. Most brand leaders are 6–12 months behind. Staying current isn't vanity; it's strategy.

4. **Human curation is the new differentiator.** As AI-generated content floods every channel, the brands that stand out will be the ones with a clear, consistent human point of view that AI can't replicate.

5. **Smaller brands have a real opportunity right now.** Enterprise AI adoption is still clunky and expensive. Agile teams using the right tools well can produce enterprise-quality work at fraction of the cost.
