## Who I am and what I write about

I am a brand strategist and AI explorer focused on how brands can practically leverage AI today — not the hype, the real applications. My audience is brand builders, marketing leaders, and creative teams navigating the AI era.

My core belief: **AI works best when humans stay in the loop.** AI drafts, researches, and executes. Humans decide, shape the voice, and take accountability.

---

## The stories I want to write about

### 1. AI Tool Launches & New Features
When the major AI tools ship something new, I want to write about it from a brand practitioner's perspective — not just what the feature does, but what it unlocks for marketing, creative, and strategy work.

**Tools I follow closely:**
- ChatGPT / OpenAI (GPT models, Operator, Sora, Deep Research)
- Claude / Anthropic (Claude Sonnet, Opus, Haiku, MCP)
- Gemini / Google DeepMind (Gemini, NotebookLM, Veo, Imagen)
- Perplexity AI (search AI, Deep Research)
- ElevenLabs (voice AI, audio generation)
- Midjourney (image generation, v6, v7)
- Runway (AI video, Gen-2, Gen-3)
- Suno / Udio (AI music generation)
- Pika Labs (video generation)
- Synthesia (AI video avatars)
- HeyGen (AI video)
- Cursor / GitHub Copilot (AI coding tools — useful for brand teams building tools)

### 2. BigTech AI Moves
When Google, Microsoft, Apple, Amazon, Meta, Adobe, Salesforce, or Nvidia announce an AI product, integration, or strategy move — especially anything that touches marketing platforms, creative suites, or productivity tools brands already use.

**Specific things I care about:**
- Microsoft Copilot updates (integrated into Office, Teams, etc.)
- Adobe Firefly and Generative Fill
- Apple Intelligence features
- Google Search AI (how it changes brand visibility)
- Meta AI across Instagram and WhatsApp
- Salesforce Agentforce (AI agents in CRM)
- Amazon Bedrock and AWS AI services
- Nvidia AI (chips and platforms powering everything above)

### 3. AI Startup Funding & Acquisitions
When an AI startup raises a significant round (Series A and above), gets acquired, or reaches a major valuation milestone — I want to write about what the investment signals about where the brand and marketing technology market is heading.

**I especially follow:**
- AI tools for content, creative, and marketing workflows
- AI infrastructure that will power tomorrow's brand tools
- Agentic AI companies (AI that can autonomously complete tasks)
- Voice AI, video AI, image AI startups
- Any startup that secures $50M+ in funding

### 4. YouTube Video Releases & Demos
When AI companies drop a major YouTube demo, product keynote, or walkthrough — I want to write about what I watched and what it means for brand practitioners. I am specifically watching channels from:
- OpenAI
- Anthropic
- Google DeepMind
- ElevenLabs
- Midjourney
- Runway
- Any viral AI demo that brand people are talking about

### 5. Human in the Loop — My Own Experiments
Posts where I share my personal experience using AI tools in real brand or marketing work:
- What I tested
- What worked and what did not
- What surprised me
- What I kept vs. what I rewrote or discarded
- The workflow I built

### 6. AI for Brands — Strategy & Commentary
Broader pieces on how brands are navigating AI:
- Companies getting it right (and what they're doing)
- Common mistakes brands are making
- The future of creative work with AI
- AI literacy for marketing teams
- Ethics and brand safety in AI content

## What I am NOT interested in

- Purely technical research papers with no brand application
- AI for healthcare, finance, or law (unless it crosses over into brand/marketing)
- General tech news unrelated to AI
- Negative AI discourse without constructive takeaways
- Posts that just summarise news — I want opinion and a clear point of view

---

## My posting goal

Show that I am **actively following and experimenting with AI** as a brand practitioner. Every post should make it clear that I:
1. Follow the news closely
2. Think about it through a brand lens
3. Actually test things, not just observe
4. Have an opinion — not just "AI is amazing" but specific takes on what matters and why

Audience: brand builders, CMOs, marketing leaders, creative directors, and agency strategists who want to understand how to navigate AI without the overwhelm.
