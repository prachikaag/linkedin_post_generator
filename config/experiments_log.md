## Experiments

## 2024-01-15 — Claude 3.5 Sonnet × Brand Voice Audit

**What I did:** Fed a full brand style guide (40 pages) and asked Claude to audit consistency across 20 existing campaign headlines.
**Setup:** Claude 3.5 Sonnet, 100K context window, single prompt with full doc + headlines
**What I expected:** A rough pass that I'd need to clean up heavily.
**What actually happened:** The audit was structurally rigorous — it correctly flagged tone inconsistencies I'd missed. It missed nuance on two headlines that required cultural context.
**Standout moment:** It called out that three "bold" headlines were actually safe by our own definition. That was the useful insight.
**Post potential:** Yes — the specific finding about bold vs. safe is a compelling angle.
**Status:** Idea only

---

## 2024-01-10 — ChatGPT-4o × Creative Brief Writing

**What I did:** Used GPT-4o to write a first-draft creative brief from a strategy deck and a client brief PDF.
**Setup:** GPT-4o, uploaded both docs, single prompt: "Write a creative brief in [format]"
**What I expected:** A serviceable first draft, 60% usable.
**What actually happened:** The tone matching was surprisingly good. The insight section was generic — it summarised rather than synthesised.
**Standout moment:** The brief wrote better CTA options than I would have in a first pass. The strategic synthesis was weaker.
**Post potential:** Yes — the split between execution (strong) vs. strategy (weak) is an honest, useful take.
**Status:** Draft written

---

## 2024-01-05 — Midjourney v6 × Brand Identity Exploration

**What I did:** Used MJ v6 to explore visual directions for a new brand identity before the concepting phase with designers.
**Setup:** Midjourney v6.1, 15 prompts across 3 conceptual territories, reference images from competitors
**What I expected:** Rough inspiration images, not presentation-ready.
**What actually happened:** 4 of 15 outputs were strong enough to include directly in a client deck as direction indicators.
**Standout moment:** The client picked a direction from MJ output that wasn't in the designers' original brief. We built the identity from that direction.
**Post potential:** Yes — the detail about client selection is the hook.
**Status:** Idea only
