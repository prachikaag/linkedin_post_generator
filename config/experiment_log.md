## Sep 2026 Claude Projects — Custom brand voice drafting
**Tool:** Claude (claude.ai/projects)
**Use case:** Setting up a project with brand kit context for drafting LinkedIn posts
**What worked:** Having the brand kit and tone guidelines in the project instructions massively cut editing time. First drafts felt like mine.
**What surprised me:** The more specific the instructions, the more it sounds like me — not generic AI.
**Brand angle:** Every brand should be running a project per campaign voice, not one generic AI account.
**Status:** ongoing

## Aug 2026 Perplexity Pages — Turning research into shareable assets
**Tool:** Perplexity Pages (perplexity.ai)
**Use case:** Compiling AI landscape research into a readable page for a client brief
**What worked:** Clean output, auto-citations, readable formatting. Saved 2 hours.
**What surprised me:** It handles nuance better than I expected — it flagged conflicting data points.
**Brand angle:** Perfect for knowledge briefs, competitive landscapes, and trend reports.
**Status:** one-time (will test again for next client brief)

## Aug 2026 ElevenLabs Voice Design — Brand voice audio prototype
**Tool:** ElevenLabs Voice Design (elevenlabs.io)
**Use case:** Prototyping a brand voice for a fictional brief
**What worked:** The granular controls (age, accent, energy) let you get surprisingly specific.
**What surprised me:** How fast you can go from brief to audio — under 10 minutes.
**Brand angle:** Brand voice is no longer just copy guidelines. It's an audio asset now.
**Status:** ongoing
