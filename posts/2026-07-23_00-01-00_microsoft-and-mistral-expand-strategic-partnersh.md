The AI race just split into three simultaneous battles.
Most brands are watching only one. 🔍

This week: Microsoft locked in a multibillion-dollar European AI deal. Google shipped three new Gemini models. Anthropic and OpenAI shattered Washington lobbying records.

**Product.** Microsoft and Mistral are deploying sovereign AI for European finance and healthcare (Microsoft AI Blog). Mistral's models are now in Azure, Copilot Studio, and Microsoft Foundry. Google's Gemini 3.6 Flash uses 17% fewer output tokens than its predecessor (TechCrunch).

**Policy.** Anthropic spent $1.97M on federal lobbying in Q2 — up 26% from Q1 (Axios). OpenAI spent $1.2M. Combined: $3.17M in a single quarter. Anthropic is donating $40M total to an AI policy group ahead of midterms (Axios). 👀

**Sovereignty.** A White House official accused Moonshot AI of distilling Anthropic's Fable model (Bloomberg). No U.S. official has publicly named a specific Chinese lab for model copying — until now.

Competitive moats are being tested from every direction.

The question is shifting. Not "which model is best?" — but "which model is approved in my market?" Finance, healthcare, and government are the new AI battlegrounds.
