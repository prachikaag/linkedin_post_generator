AI stopped being a tool. It became a teammate.

Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 self-corrects and recovers from errors without intervention. 🤖 It brings near-frontier performance at half the price of its flagship model.

Google's Gemini Spark emails itself tasks and executes them overnight — on dedicated cloud VMs. Meta's Muse Spark 1.2 handles entire code repositories in a single 1M-context window.

Three companies, three bets on the same insight. The era of autonomous AI agents has arrived.

Agentic AI marketing platforms are now mainstream — not experimental. Nearly a third of enterprise software is expected to include agentic AI by 2028. Salesforce's Agentforce and Typeface already give teams full campaign execution via AI.

Here's what worries me, though.

Most brands are deploying these tools without the governance layer they need. Who approves what the agent does? What are the escalation rules?

The EU AI Act activated on August 2. 🌍 AI systems must now identify themselves when interacting with humans. That's a start — but accountability doesn't stop there.

Anthropic raised $65B this year at a near-$1 trillion valuation. OpenAI crossed one billion users. The pace of change is only accelerating.

The real question for brands isn't "which agent should I use?" It's "who is accountable for what the agent does?"

Get the governance right first. Then deploy.
