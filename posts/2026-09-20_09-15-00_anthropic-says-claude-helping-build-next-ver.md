Anthropic's Claude now leads 26% of the company's own AI research. In February, that number was zero.

Seven months. That's how long it took to go from "AI assists our R&D" to "AI leads a quarter of it — end to end."
90% of Anthropic's work now involves Claude as an active collaborator.

Dario Amodei announced this publicly — and then used the moment to challenge the rest of the industry. 📣
He called on every AI lab to publish similar metrics openly.
That's not a product update. That's a transparency gauntlet.

The numbers around Anthropic right now are striking.
The company pushed its IPO from October to November 2026, targeting a $2 trillion valuation.
It named Accenture's Faculty unit as its first embedded AI evaluator — both companies pledging $1 billion each.
Anthropic is pacing toward $100 billion in annualized revenue this year. 📈

Here's what the 26% number actually tells me:
The companies building AI are using AI to build AI — and that feedback loop is already compounding.
Not a future scenario. A current one.

For teams using AI tools today: the version you're experimenting with is being improved by a version of itself.
The pace of capability change is accelerating, not stabilizing. ✨
The gap between teams building AI into their work now and teams waiting for the "finished" product — that gap is growing faster than it appears.

How much of your work do you currently do with AI — and has that percentage changed in the last six months?
