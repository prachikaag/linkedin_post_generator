AI is now funding itself — startups built to improve AI itself.
This pattern became undeniable this week. 📈

Venture capital is flooding into AI infrastructure and agentic tools.
The frontier has shifted from models to autonomous deployment systems.

Recursive Superintelligence emerged from stealth with $650M at a $4.65B valuation. [1]
Founded by Richard Socher, former Salesforce Chief Scientist, and Peter Norvig.
Their goal: build AI that finds its own weaknesses.
Then have it rewrite itself — no humans needed.

Runway hit a $5.3B valuation this quarter. [2]
It added $40M in annual recurring revenue in Q2 2026.
Runway is no longer just video — it is now building world models.

Writer launched AI agents that act without waiting for a prompt. [3]
They respond autonomously to events in Gmail, Slack, and Google Drive. 🤖

The capital is not just chasing models. It is chasing systems that act.

HBR argues marketers now need a brand code — a machine-readable knowledge base. [4]
Agents need to know your strategy. Not just your tone guide.
Gartner predicts 60% of brands will use agentic AI for customer interactions by 2028. [5]

For marketers, this is the moment to build foundations. 🎯
Not the agents — the structured data those agents will run on.
Messy brand assets will be amplified at scale. Not fixed.

Anthropic launched Claude for Small Business — now inside Quickbooks, PayPal, HubSpot, and Canva. [6]
Agentic AI is not just for Fortune 500 teams anymore.

Where is your strategy structured well enough for an AI agent to act on it?
