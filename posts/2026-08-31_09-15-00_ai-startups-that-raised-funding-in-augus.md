AI just had its biggest funding month on record.
The numbers behind it are something else. 🚀

In H1 2026, global VC funding hit $510 billion.
AI startups alone claimed $407 billion — more than all of 2025 combined.
Capital is flooding every layer of the stack.

Fireworks AI closed a $1.5 billion Series D at a $17.5 billion valuation.
Together AI raised $800 million Series C.
Emerald AI hit unicorn status via a $150 million Series A.

Nvidia reportedly agreed to acquire Hugging Face — used by 13 million developers — for $12.9 billion. 📊
DeepSeek is reportedly closing a $7.4 billion round at a $74 billion valuation.
A 2027 IPO is on the table.

Here's what I think brands are missing... 💡
This isn't scattered enthusiasm — it's a structural shift.
Investment is moving away from model training and toward inference and enterprise tooling.

Nvidia acquiring Hugging Face isn't just a chip play.
It's a bid to own the developer ecosystem — the distribution layer for AI.

For marketing and brand teams, this matters more than it looks.
The infrastructure being funded now shapes the tools available in 12 months.
Watch which categories attract serious capital — that's your forward map.
