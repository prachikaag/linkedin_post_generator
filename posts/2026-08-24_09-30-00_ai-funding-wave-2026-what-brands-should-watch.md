$407B.

That's how much AI startups raised in H1 2026 alone — more than all of 2025.

The money isn't going to chatbots. It's going to the layer beneath them.

Fireworks AI closed a $1.505B Series D. Together AI pulled $800M Series C. OLIX Computing raised $312M for photonic inference chips. Higgsfield AI raised $400M at a $5.4B valuation, backed by Goldman Sachs and Intel. Annualized revenue already at $700M. 🎬

ElevenLabs expanded beyond text-to-speech into voice agents, dubbing, and music. Audio AI is becoming a reusable business layer. Anthropic is reportedly preparing for an IPO. Midjourney is building its own data center for a scaled-up V9 model. 🎙️

Here's what brands are missing: the opportunity isn't which app to pick. It's which AI layer will power your next product.

Inference, video, audio, and agents are moving from experiment to infrastructure. Perplexity launched Computer — agentic tasks delivered via email, no new app required. Anthropic shipped a Concise output mode for Claude Code in direct response to user feedback. 📡

Capital is telling us where AI is maturing. Infrastructure wins before apps do.

Brands and marketers: stop comparing chatbots. Ask which modality — audio, video, or agents — fits your customer journey. That's where the next 12 months will be won.

Which AI capability matters most to your brand right now — video, audio, or agents?
