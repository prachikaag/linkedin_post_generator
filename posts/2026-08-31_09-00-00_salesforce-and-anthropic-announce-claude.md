Enterprise software just got turned inside out. 🔄

The AI landscape is shifting — and not just at the model level. Two significant moves recently reshaped how enterprise platforms will work.

Salesforce and Anthropic launched Claudeforce. It puts 37 prebuilt sales skills inside Claude's interface. (Salesforce News / VentureBeat)

Claude now powers Salesforce's Agentforce reasoning engine as the primary front-end. CEO Marc Benioff addressed investor fears about AI replacing CRM software. His answer: go deeper into AI, not away from it. (CNBC)

Separately, OpenAI expanded ChatGPT ads to 31 European markets. Free users see ads. Paid accounts stay clean. This follows rollouts across 8 other markets. (OpenAI Blog / Digiday) 📢

AI model releases have quadrupled since 2023. Claude Opus 5, GPT-5.6, and Gemini 3.7 all launched this summer. More than 362 models are now tracked. (Oreate AI)

Here's what I think brands are missing: platform lock-in is back. And it's structural now — not superficial.

Salesforce isn't competing with AI. It's fusing with it. The model is the interface.

AI business models are splitting by tier. Enterprise users get embedded, governed workflows. Free users get contextual ads.

If you're evaluating enterprise tools this year, ask which AI is inside. That decision shapes your stack for years. 💡

ChatGPT's European ad expansion is a new ad placement. Study it now — before it scales further.

Are you tracking these platform bets — or still treating AI tools as interchangeable?
