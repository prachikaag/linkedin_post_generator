Two AI startups raised $600M+ this week — neither is building a chatbot.

Vertical AI is where the biggest bets are landing. Investors aren't backing general AI anymore — they want domain depth and proven ROI.

EliseAI raised $350M at a $4B valuation, led by a16z and Bessemer. The platform powers 1 in 6 U.S. apartments. $200M ARR. Five straight years of 100% growth. Armadin raised $255.5M Series B, co-led by a16z and Accel, at a $2.5B+ valuation. It builds AI that finds security vulnerabilities at machine speed. CEO Kevin Mandia warned that AI lets attackers outpace any human defense team.

The pattern: generic AI is maturing — the high-growth bets are vertical. These companies own specific workflows in industries with legacy debt. That's the moat.

For brand leaders: study which sectors are getting the most AI funding. Housing, healthcare, cybersecurity — those are where ROI is proven. Ask where your vertical advantage intersects with AI.
