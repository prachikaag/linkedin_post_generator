The open-source AI wave just hit a new milestone. 🚀

A 2.4 trillion parameter model is now publicly available.

Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen3.8-Max — the largest open-weight AI model ever trained. Not a research demo. Real competition for proprietary giants.

Google's Gemini 3.7 Flash recently launched at $0.75 per million input tokens. With a 1 million token context window. Now powering most of Google's production API traffic.

And the money is following the infrastructure. Fireworks AI just closed $1.505 billion — the biggest AI startup round of the month. Together AI secured $800 million to expand its open-source platform. Global AI investment is on pace to exceed $120 billion in 2026. More than double 2024 totals. 💰

Here's what I think brands are missing...

The race isn't about benchmark scores anymore. It's about speed, cost, and access. Open-weight models are closing the gap — fast. Brands now have serious, affordable alternatives to proprietary APIs.

The question isn't whether open-source AI is good enough. It already is — for many use cases.

So what should your team do now?

Inference costs are falling. Capable models are becoming affordable. Your AI stack from last year may already be outdated. Start re-evaluating.
