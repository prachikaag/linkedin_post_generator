The same week Anthropic went public at a valuation larger than most national economies, the AI industry released more capable models at lower prices than ever before.

Five major model releases in two weeks. Anthropic's IPO raised $81.3 billion on Nasdaq — the largest tech public offering in history. OpenAI dropped GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol on November 17, with Astra priced 20% below its predecessor. Google followed with Gemini 4 Neon. Claude Mythos 5.2 and Fable 5.2 had already landed on November 9. 🚀

An API price war is now emerging between OpenAI and Google.

Here's what I think brands are missing.

The story isn't just "more models." It's that the AI infrastructure layer is maturing — getting cheaper, more capable, and publicly accountable in one move. When the maker of Claude goes public at a trillion-dollar valuation while simultaneously cutting prices, scale has arrived.

The question is no longer "should we use AI?" It's "which model for which job?"

For brands, the emerging price war is a gift. Embedding AI into more workflows is becoming financially viable. 🎯

But with more models comes more complexity. Build your internal evaluation rubric now, before the choices overwhelm your team.
