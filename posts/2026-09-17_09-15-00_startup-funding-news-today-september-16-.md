$330 million. That's how much AI startups raised in roughly 48 hours this week.

Two deals define where AI investment is heading in Q3 2026.

Anew Labs, spun out of ByteDance, closed a $290M raise at a $1.5B valuation on September 16. ByteDance retains 56%. The focus: AI drug discovery — protein structure prediction, molecular design, and antibody engineering. This is AI moving from language into biology. 🔬

TypeSafe AI emerged from stealth the same week with a $40M seed round led by DCVC. Founded by Diogo Almeida, co-inventor of RLHF and a key architect of ChatGPT, TypeSafe is building machine-native AI — models designed as software primitives, not chat interfaces.

Meanwhile, Salesforce launched seven named AI agents ahead of Dreamforce and reported 7 billion Agentic Work Units delivered across Agentforce and Slack — 3.2 billion in Q2 alone. Enterprise AI adoption is no longer theoretical. 📊

The pattern here isn't hype. It's infrastructure.

AI is moving from the chat window into the stack — embedded in biotech pipelines, enterprise CRM, and B2B software workflows. The startups raising now are building the tools your brand's stack will run on in 18 months.

For marketing and brand leaders: know these names now. The vendors your team will be evaluating next year are getting funded today.

Where in your current workflow are you most ready to embed AI — not just use it?
