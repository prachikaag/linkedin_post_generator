The biggest IPO in history just happened — and it's an AI company. 🤯

Anthropic priced its shares at $83 on November 19. It raised $81.3 billion, beating SpaceX's record haul earlier this year. Shares opened at $111.50 — a 34% jump at the bell.

Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley led the deal. Anthropic now trades on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. Reported valuations on debut day ranged from $1.5 trillion to $2 trillion. Each outlet measured at a different moment in a volatile session.

This isn't just a milestone for one company. 📈 Public markets are now willing to price frontier AI at this scale. Anthropic's Claude has become core infrastructure for a generation of products and teams. That changes how the company operates.

It answers to shareholders now. Roadmaps tie to quarterly earnings. Revenue growth becomes the main story — not just model capability.

Brands using Claude-powered tools should watch this shift closely. 👀 Pricing, features, and enterprise priorities can all move when quarterly pressure arrives. This is the moment AI tooling grows up.
