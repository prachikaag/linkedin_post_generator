Meta released the weights for an 816-billion-parameter model last week.

You can download them. Run it on your own hardware. The era of locally-hosted frontier AI just got closer. 🔓

We're watching open-weight and closed-source AI converge at speed.

GPT-6 is now the default for all ChatGPT users. Claude Haiku 5.5 launched October 7 at $0.10 per million input tokens. And now Meta has released Muse Spark Open — 816 billion parameters, free to download.

Meta built this model natively for Apple silicon. Two Mac Studios with 512GB of RAM each can run it. That's roughly $38,000 of hardware — large, but not enterprise-unthinkable. 💻

Apple updated its MLX machine learning framework to support it the same day. The model activates about 42 billion parameters per step. Frontier performance at practical compute cost.

Three things happened this week that don't look connected but are.

A free frontier model. A new default for a billion users. The cheapest enterprise-grade Claude yet.

The capability ceiling is rising. The cost floor is dropping. This is what a mature AI market looks like — not one winner, but an entire field moving together.

Brands waiting for "good enough and affordable" AI — that moment is now.
