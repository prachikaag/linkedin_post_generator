Google, Anthropic, ElevenLabs, and Amazon all moved together.

Each is pushing AI deeper into everyday work — coding to manufacturing to marketing.

It's now the default for high-volume work.
Now live across Search, AI Studio and the Gemini API — $1.50 per million input tokens.
