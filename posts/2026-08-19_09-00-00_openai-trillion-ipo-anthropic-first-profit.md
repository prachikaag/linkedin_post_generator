A $1 trillion IPO — while losing $14 billion a year.

That's the AI moment we're in right now. 💰

OpenAI is targeting a trillion-dollar valuation for its IPO despite massive annual losses. Anthropic reported its first-ever profit. And enterprise money is quietly moving toward open-source infrastructure.

Together AI's $800M round crossed $1.15B in annual bookings. Customers cut inference costs by up to 60x on open-weight models.

Fireworks AI raised $1.5B at $17.5B post-money, with 5x revenue growth year-over-year. 📈

Stripe acquired OpenRouter — $7B+ for routing to 400+ AI models for 8 million developers. Cerebras and OpenAI launched 750-token-per-second processing, 14x faster than standard. Global AI investment is on pace to exceed $120B this year.

The race is no longer about who builds the best model. It's about who controls the infrastructure running them. 🧠

Open-source inference is winning enterprise trust. Consolidation is accelerating — Stripe + OpenRouter is the proof.

Brands: watch the infrastructure layer, not just model releases. AI costs are about to drop significantly. Build workflows now — they'll get faster and cheaper as this layer matures.
