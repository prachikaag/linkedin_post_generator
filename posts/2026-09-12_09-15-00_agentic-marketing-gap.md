91% of marketers say they use AI. Fewer than a third are doing anything powerful with it. 📊

AI agents are no longer a future concept. They're running in production across enterprises right now.

Over 57% of companies have AI agents actively deployed. Most of that adoption happens outside marketing.

Gartner predicts 40% of enterprise apps will embed AI agents by end of 2026. That's 8× growth from under 5% in 2025. (Accelirate)

Just 34% of enterprise marketing teams now run an autonomous agent in production. That's double the rate from Q4 2025. (Azumo)

The other two-thirds use AI mainly for drafting and brainstorming.

Traffic to US retail sites from AI sources jumped 393% year-over-year in Q1 2026 alone. (Omnibound AI)

Analysts call it the "self-optimising marketing stack" — agents that coordinate without human direction. 🤖

---

The marketers I see struggling treat AI as a better search bar.

The ones pulling ahead build systems — agents that research, draft, schedule, and learn together.

A real capability gap is forming. Teams who get this are already pulling ahead.

I've spent months building agentic workflows in brand strategy. The productivity difference isn't incremental. It's structural.

---

Don't count your AI usage. Count your AI workflows.

The question for marketing leaders isn't "do we use AI?" It's "what does our AI do while we sleep?" 🎯
