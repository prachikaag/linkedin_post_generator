Seven new AI models in three weeks. Three of them dropped in the same week. 🤯

Something unusual happened in early October. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google all made major moves within days of each other — and the headline isn't just capability. It's price.

Anthropic launched Claude Haiku 5.5 on October 7 at $0.10 per million input tokens — 75% cheaper than its predecessor. Same week, OpenAI pushed GPT-6 with "Intelligent UI": responses that render charts, calculators, and interactive tools as the model thinks.

Gemini 4 Argon moved to general availability on October 13. It had been gated to vetted cybersecurity partners since September 30 — now it powers Google's new agentic workflows at $2 per million input tokens through November.

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash followed on October 17. Alibaba's Qwen3.6 closed out the week on October 21.

The real story here isn't any single launch. It's that the cost floor for enterprise-grade AI just dropped dramatically.

Haiku 5.5 at $0.10/M tokens makes AI classification, summarisation, and multi-agent tasks financially viable at scales most brands couldn't justify six months ago. And when the cheapest tier gets smarter and cheaper at once... the argument for "we'll wait until AI matures" runs out of road.

For brand and marketing teams, this is the moment to revisit use cases you shelved because of API costs. 📋

What felt expensive in 2025 may now be negligible. The operational question shifts from "can we afford AI?" to "what do we automate first?"

Which AI model is your team actually running in production right now — and are you still paying last year's prices?
