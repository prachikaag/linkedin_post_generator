Three frontier AI models launched within weeks. The agentic era just got a lot more competitive. 🧠

Claude Sonnet 5, GPT-5.6, and DeepSeek V4 Flash all launched in rapid succession. The battle isn't about raw intelligence anymore. It's about which model can reliably finish a multi-step job — without constant supervision.

Anthropic launched Sonnet 5 on June 30. Stronger tool use, multi-step coherence, 1M-token context window. The next day, Microsoft made it GA in Azure Foundry for enterprise production. [1, 2]

DeepSeek's V4 Flash went official on July 31. It scored 82.7 on Terminal-Bench — matching or beating V4 Pro on agent benchmarks. At a fraction of the cost. [3]

OpenAI released GPT-5.6 in three flavors: Sol, Luna, Terra — each tuned for different speed and power tradeoffs. [4]

Every one of these launches is framed as "agentic." That's the real story. The question has shifted: not "is it smart?" but "can it finish the job unsupervised?"

The Claude Sonnet 5 agentic enterprise deployment story is the most telling. Going GA in Azure clears a real procurement barrier. Enterprise teams can now build production agents without a separate Anthropic contract. [2]
