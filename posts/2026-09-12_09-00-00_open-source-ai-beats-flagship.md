Open-source AI just beat its own flagship model. That should be getting more attention. 🔍

The gap between open and closed AI is collapsing faster than most teams realize.

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash dropped this week — open weights, MIT license, free on Hugging Face.

This isn't a hobbyist release. It's a 552B Mixture-of-Experts model built for production-grade work.

Tests across multiple independent labs confirm V4.1 Flash outperforms DeepSeek V4-Pro — its larger flagship. Gains show up in speed, performance, and cost. (SiliconAngle)

Pricing: $0.30 per million input tokens. $1.20 per million output. Off-peak rates drop both in half.
