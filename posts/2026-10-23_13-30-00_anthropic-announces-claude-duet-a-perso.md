The AI assistant era is ending. The proactive agent era has started. 🤖

This week alone: Anthropic, Meta, OpenAI, and Microsoft all have personal agents in market. The category moved from demo to daily use — fast.

Anthropic's Claude Duet launched as a research preview this week. ☀️
It sends a morning brief across your connected apps. Checks in through the day. Messages you only when something needs action.

Before any major move — payment, contract, deletion — it asks first. That's the human-in-the-loop design that matters most right now.

The same week, Walmart opened its platform to AI agents. 🛒
An MCP server lets ChatGPT, Gemini, and Meta's Muse search inventory. They can buy on a customer's behalf. Amazon reportedly blocked third-party agents from doing the same. Two retailers, opposite bets on the same trend.

One early review called Duet "the first personal agent I'd call a pro tool." That's a signal.

This isn't a feature update. It's a behaviour shift. 🔄
These agents don't wait. They monitor, flag, and act within set boundaries.

For marketing teams, the question has changed. It's no longer "should we use AI?" It's "who is my customer's agent — and am I building for how it searches?"

The playbook for reaching humans and for reaching their agents aren't the same.

Which proactive agent have you tried — and what changed in how you work?
