The AI pricing war just escalated — and brands should pay close attention.

Three major labs shipped new models this month. The story isn't just the performance gains. It's how fast the cost floor is dropping.

OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Terra — released just three days ago — matches GPT-6 Astra at one-tenth the cost per token. OpenAI also launched Dots, always-on AI agents that stay active in the background.

Anthropic recently refreshed its entire model lineup. Claude Sonnet 5.5 scores 70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, up from 10.3% for its predecessor. It topped Artificial Analysis' Coding Agent Index at launch. Claude Haiku 5.5 runs roughly 75% cheaper than the version it replaced. Google shipped Gemini 4 Argon in the same wave. 🤖

Here's what I think brands are missing — this isn't a performance race. It's a cost collapse.

Getting top-tier intelligence at a fraction of last year's price changes what's viable at scale. Background agents like Dots signal something bigger: AI that doesn't wait to be prompted. It just runs.

For marketing teams, two things follow. Cost is no longer the reason to hold back on intelligent automation. Agentic workflows deserve serious attention now — not next year.

What are you watching most closely: the price drops, the agentic shift, or something else entirely?
