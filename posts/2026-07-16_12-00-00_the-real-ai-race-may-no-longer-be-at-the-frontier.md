Everyone's watching the frontier model race.
The real action is happening beneath it. 🤔

Open-weight models just made a major move.
Chinese models now lead Hugging Face downloads — for the first time.
That's 41% of all downloads, passing US models.

Six of OpenRouter's top models come from Chinese firms.
Meta's VP warned enterprises: you have ~20 months to rebuild your stack.
A former OpenAI CTO released Inkling — a 975B open-weight model. 🚀
Meta killed a token-spend leaderboard after engineers competed to burn billions.
Cohere says enterprise AI requires full-stack control, not just a closed API.

The "which API is best?" debate misses the bigger story.
Open models shift who controls AI — fundamentally.
Brands locked into closed APIs today may be rebuilding in 18 months. ⚠️

Before signing any vendor deal, ask: can we self-host this?
Open-weight options deserve a real seat at the table. 💡

Open or closed for enterprise AI — where do you stand?
