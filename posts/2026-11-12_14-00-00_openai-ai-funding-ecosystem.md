OpenAI wired $11 billion — still $19 billion short of its own $30B target. 💰

The AI funding machine is loud right now. But the numbers tell a more interesting story.

OpenAI closed part of a new round at a $1.25T valuation. That's down from an initial target of $1.5T.

The broader ecosystem didn't wait.

---

Rox just raised $100M in Series B. ⚡ This AI sales startup is now valued at $1.3B — targeting Salesforce directly.

UK chip startup Fractile closed a $720M Series C. The UK government's Sovereign AI Fund backed them.

Thinking Machines Lab raised $5 billion in a seed round. Let that sink in — a seed round.

Google shipped Gemini 4 Carbon on November 10. OpenAI released GPT Image 2.6 and is merging ChatGPT's tabs.

---

Here's what I keep thinking about — the shortfall matters. 🤔

OpenAI wanted $30B and got $11B. That gap is telling.

Appetite for foundation model bets is cooling at the top. But application-layer plays — like Rox targeting Salesforce — are heating up.

The smart money is moving down the stack, into tools that touch real workflows.

---

For brands, this shift is worth watching. 👀

The startups getting funded now will reach your teams soon.

Knowing which layer is getting backed helps you spot where to focus.
