More AI capital was raised in H1 2026 than in all of 2025.

That's not a trend. That's a structural shift. 📊

Global venture funding hit $510B in just six months — a record.
The US AI startup funding surge has concentrated into two names: OpenAI and Anthropic.

Together they pulled in $217B — 43% of all global VC this half.

Amazon is spending $200B on AI infrastructure in 2026 alone.
Their Trainium chip business already runs at $20B annual revenue.
OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, and Uber have committed $225B in orders to Amazon.

Meanwhile, the AI inference price war is reshaping the landscape.
China's Moonshot AI launched Kimi K3 — wiping $3.3T from chip stocks in days.
Moonshot is now targeting a $30B IPO. It was valued at $4B months ago.

Lovable is in talks to double its valuation to $13.2B after hitting $500M ARR.

Here's what I think brands are missing...

Two companies holding 43% of global VC means platform dependency risk — at scale.
If either stumbles, every tool and workflow built on top goes with it.

The AI inference price war sounds like good news. It won't benefit everyone equally.
Large enterprises negotiate pricing. Smaller teams pay list.

Audit your AI vendor stack now — before pricing changes force your hand. ⚠️
Capital concentration shapes costs, product direction, and long-term reliability.
