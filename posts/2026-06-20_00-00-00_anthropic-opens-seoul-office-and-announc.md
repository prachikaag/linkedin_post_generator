The AI buildout isn't slowing down — it's going global. 🌏

This week, Anthropic opened its Seoul office — its third in Asia-Pacific. NAVER, Samsung SDS, LG CNS, and Nexon are rolling out Claude company-wide.

Meanwhile, Anthropic launched Enterprise-Managed Authorization for Claude's MCP connectors. IT admins provision AI integrations once — through Okta. No individual OAuth screens. Seven providers at launch: Asana, Atlassian, Canva, Figma, Granola, Linear, and Supabase. 🔐

The funding picture tells the same story. AI attracted $242 billion — 80% of global venture capital in Q1 2026. Anthropic raised $30B; OpenAI raised $122B that same period. Series A rounds for AI startups now average $51.9M.

But it's not all expansion. ⚠️ The US government recently ordered Anthropic to disable Fable 5 and Mythos 5. Both models are offline for foreign nationals. The DOJ is appealing the court pause.

GPT-5.5 is now generally available on Microsoft Foundry for enterprise teams. ElevenLabs also expanded its audio platform with new agent controls this week. The AI release cadence is relentless.

Here's what I think brands are missing: the infrastructure layer. Identity, access controls, model selection — these decisions are happening now. They will shape your AI strategy for years.

Enterprise IT teams are becoming AI gatekeepers. Worth paying attention to.
