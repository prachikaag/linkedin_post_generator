AI pricing just crossed a threshold. It may not come back.

OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Luna prices by 80% — to $0.20 per million input tokens. 💡 ChatGPT now serves over one billion active users weekly.

Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 brings near-flagship performance at the same price as its predecessor. A new effort dial lets you balance cost and capability per task.

Google's Gemini Spark runs as a 24/7 cloud agent for $99.99/month. It handles emails, builds itineraries, reconciles invoices — while you sleep.

The frontier model pricing war is real. Every major AI company is dropping costs while adding capability.

Here's what I think brands are missing...

This isn't about the models. It's about who owns the workflows. The companies winning aren't the ones with the best benchmarks. They're the ones already embedded in how your team works.

Anthropic raised $65B this year at a $965B valuation. OpenAI surpassed one billion users. Neither talks about margins right now. That tells you something.

The EU AI Act activated on August 2. 🌍 Compliance is now a real cost for high-risk AI deployments.

For brands: the cost barrier to AI is essentially gone. The new barrier is judgment. Which tasks do you delegate to AI, and which do you keep human?

What's your team doing differently now that frontier AI costs almost nothing per query?
