AI agents are now running inside your company's tools. Nobody asked your team. 🤖

October 2026 has been a turning point for workplace AI.

Google launched a general-purpose work agent on October 8. It can run for hours — spanning Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, and your command line. Microsoft Copilot reached 20 million paid enterprise seats. OpenAI's Presence platform began rolling out to select enterprises in July.

The numbers are blunt.

Salesforce reportedly cut support headcount from roughly 9,000 to 5,000. Agents now handle the rest. Across 52 companies this year, nearly 183,000 employees were affected by AI-linked layoffs.

But the counterpoint matters too. A Randstad study from October 30 found AI is reshaping roles, not eliminating them. Job openings requiring generative AI skills jumped 135% last year.

The real pattern isn't "AI replaces workers." It's "AI replaces workflows." And then headcounts adjust around the new normal — quietly, gradually.

Three platforms — Google, Microsoft, OpenAI — are racing to own your workday. That's a shift in power beyond product features. Worth paying attention to.

If you lead a brand or marketing team, start mapping which workflows are agent-ready. Not to cut people — but to understand where agents can own the repeatable. That frees your team for what genuinely needs human judgment.

Is your team already working alongside AI agents — or is that still a future plan?
