Anthropic is about to become a public company. 🏦

The numbers behind it are unlike anything in AI history.

The company filed its IPO publicly on October 26. Banks put the target valuation between $1.5 and $2 trillion. That would surpass SpaceX's record-breaking June debut.

The S-1 amendment filed November 2 shows Q3 revenue of $16.96 billion. That's up 47% in a single quarter.

Meanwhile, Claude keeps shipping fast. 🚀

Personal Finance launched November 5. Claude Duet — a proactive agent working alongside you — launched October 21. Claude Opus 4.6 now handles 1 million tokens and coordinates Agent Teams.

Here's what I think brands are missing...

Anthropic isn't building a chatbot. It is building AI infrastructure with financial tools, enterprise agents, and a growing marketplace. The IPO is the capstone — not the story.

The pattern: Claude is moving from assistant to platform. That is a different question than "which AI tool should we use?"

For brand and marketing teams: watch Claude Marketplace closely. These launches aren't demos. They're the shape of how work gets done in 12 months. 📋

Build AI fluency now. When Claude becomes public infrastructure, you want your team fluent — not catching up.
