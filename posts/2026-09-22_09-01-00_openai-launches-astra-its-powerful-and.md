GPT-6 Astra can now use your computer without you guiding it. 🖥️

OpenAI launched GPT-6 Astra on September 3. It navigates computers, browsers, and workflows like a person. That same week, three other frontier models hit major new benchmarks.

The model race has moved from impressive demos to actually doing the work.

Astra is OpenAI's first model to reach Critical cybersecurity status. It found exploits in well-protected systems with no human guidance.

Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.1 raised the bar for coding and knowledge work. A 75% cache-read cost cut signals that production deployment is now the goal. 📉

Gemini 3.8 Flash is Google's third Flash in six weeks. It beats most larger frontier models on long-horizon software engineering.

Cognition's Devin coding agent grew from $492M to $900M in run-rate revenue since May.

I think we're past the benchmark-chasing phase. These models are built for deployment, not headlines.

The cost cuts, agent focus, and revenue growth all point the same direction. AI is moving from assistant to autonomous operator.

For brands and marketing teams, the window to figure out AI agents is narrowing. 🧭

The teams that start now will have workflow advantages that are hard to close later.

Which capability will change marketing first: computer use, autonomous coding, or AI inside ads?
