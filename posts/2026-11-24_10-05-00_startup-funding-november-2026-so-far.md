$38.2 billion raised in AI — in a single month. 💰

November 2026 has set a new pace for AI startup funding. Three rounds alone total more than $2.5 billion. The numbers keep climbing.

Fal raised $1.3B in a Series F in early November. Nooks closed a $500M growth round on November 4, led by General Catalyst. Mercor raised $750M at a $20 billion valuation on November 10. In all, 371 startups raised in November so far — $38.2 billion in disclosed capital.

These aren't scattered bets. 🧠 Fal builds media generation infrastructure for developers. Mercor builds AI-driven data annotation and training pipelines. Nooks is an AI-native sales platform. Capital is flowing to AI that fits inside real commercial workflows.

The pattern is consistent. Investors aren't funding experiments — they're funding infrastructure. The winners here are vertical, specific, and deeply integrated.

The tools your team uses in 18 months are being built right now. 📌 Watch these infrastructure plays closely. The capital signals where enterprise AI is actually landing.
