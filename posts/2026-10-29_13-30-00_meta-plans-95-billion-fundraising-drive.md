$95 billion. That's what Meta alone is raising for AI infrastructure.

October 2026 is shaping up as a historic month for AI investment. Across 66 rounds, AI startups raised $42.1 billion — led by OpenAI's $20 billion raise. Week after week, the biggest deals trace back to AI.

Meta launched $35 billion in stock offerings recently, with another $60 billion planned for 2027. Nvidia, AMD, and Broadcom are all buying in.

But it's not only the giants. Pathos AI raised $237M at a $3B valuation — expanding from oncology into Alzheimer's research. Salience Labs pulled $125M for AI hardware. In one week, the top 10 rounds were almost entirely AI — totaling $5.18B.

Year-old startups are hitting unicorn status before Series C. That's not momentum — that's the market repricing AI entirely. 🚀

Here's what I think brands are missing...

This isn't about who builds the best model. It's about the infrastructure your tools will run on.

Capital is flowing into AI hardware, health platforms, and enterprise systems — not consumer apps. Foundations are being poured right now.

If you run a brand or marketing team — audit your AI dependencies. Who controls the tools you rely on?

The companies raising billions today are building the rails. Brands on those rails need a strategy, not just a subscription.
