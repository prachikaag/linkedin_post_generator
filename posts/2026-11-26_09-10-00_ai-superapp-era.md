The AI assistant era is over. The superapp era is here.

Both OpenAI and Anthropic are merging chat and agentic tools into single experiences. This week alone, the direction became impossible to ignore.

OpenAI doubled ChatGPT Pro usage limits this week. It's also folding Work features into the main Chat interface. One hub for chat, code, and AI-assisted work. 🔧

Anthropic recently merged Claude Chat and Claude Cowork into one unified app. Pro and Max subscribers get it first. Two products, now one environment.

Meanwhile, capital is following conviction. Manus AI raised $500M recently, doubling its valuation to $4 billion. It's now China's most valuable AI agent company. The money isn't going to chat — it's going to agents.

Here's what I think brands are missing...

This isn't a UI refresh. When AI becomes the environment you work inside, everything changes. The way teams brief, build, and review — all of it needs to evolve.

The question isn't which AI tool to use. It's how to redesign workflows for an AI-native workday.

If your team still uses AI for one-off tasks, the gap is growing fast. Start asking: what if AI handled the first pass of everything? 👀

How is your team thinking about this shift — from AI tools to AI-native workflows?
