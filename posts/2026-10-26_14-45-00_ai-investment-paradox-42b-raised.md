$42.5 billion raised for AI in October alone.
Yet 76% of marketing leaders spend 3+ hours every week fixing AI output by hand.

Something doesn't add up. 📈

The money flows upstream — to model builders and infrastructure.
The friction lives downstream, with the teams trying to use it day-to-day.

October 2026 saw 75 AI deals totaling $42.5B, per aifunding.me. 📊
OpenAI raised $20B at a $122B valuation. That's just one round.
Meanwhile, $74.1B has flowed into AI funding this year — surpassing all of 2025.
96% of the world's largest brand owners now use generative or agentic AI in marketing.

But here's what I keep coming back to.
76% of marketing leaders still spend 3+ hours per week editing AI output — Blazehive, 2026.
A Bentley-Gallup study found 62% of Americans reject AI-created voices in ads — even when disclosed.
Look closer: "96% adoption" means tried it, not trusted it to run unattended.

The gap isn't a model problem. It's a trust and workflow problem.
Brands winning here won't be the fastest adopters.
They'll be the ones who build the right human-in-the-loop systems first.

Are you in the 76% still editing AI output? What would close that gap for your team?
