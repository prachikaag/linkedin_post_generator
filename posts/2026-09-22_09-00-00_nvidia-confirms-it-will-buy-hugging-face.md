In one week, Nvidia agreed to buy Hugging Face for $12.9 billion. 🤯

Three frontier models launched in three days. Claude Fable 5.1, Gemini 3.8 Flash, GPT-6 Astra.

The AI ecosystem is consolidating and accelerating at once.

Nvidia's deal puts 3 million models and 18 million developers under one chip maker.

Cognition AI raised $2 billion at a $48 billion valuation. 💸 Its Devin coding agent's run-rate revenue nearly doubled to $900M since May.

Mistral closed a €3 billion Series D — the largest equity raise in European tech history.

Google launched agentic AI marketing tools at Marketing Live. One tool embeds an AI agent inside ads to answer shopper questions directly.

What I keep thinking about: control is concentrating fast.

Truly independent open-source AI is fading. Nvidia owning Hugging Face means compute and model access are converging in one company.

That reshapes the power dynamics for every team building on "open" platforms.

For brands and marketers, this is the real question.

Your AI stack runs on someone else's infrastructure. 🔍 Who owns that layer is not just a tech question — it's a brand strategy question.

Does Nvidia controlling the world's biggest open-source AI hub worry you — or feel inevitable?
