AI isn't just moving fast — it's restructuring how companies work. 🤖

The past few weeks brought a wave of launches across the AI space.

Microsoft Build, Anthropic's Asia expansion, enterprise MCP integrations — it's all pointing one way.

GPT-5.5 recently became generally available in Microsoft Foundry. [1][5]

Claude Sonnet 4.5 and Opus 4.1 also landed in Foundry preview. [1]

Anthropic launched Enterprise-Managed Authorization for MCP connectors yesterday. IT teams provision AI integrations once through Okta. No individual OAuth screens. [3]

Q1 2026 set an all-time venture record: $300B globally. AI captured 80% of it. [4] 📈

Microsoft Agent 365 and GitHub Copilot are now control planes. MCP via Okta makes AI agents enterprise-ready.

But governance is lagging badly. The US government recently banned Anthropic's top models over security concerns. [6]

Here's what brands are missing: the agent layer is being built right now.

Who controls it shapes how work gets done.

Stop asking which AI tool to use. Start asking who governs your AI agents — and how.
