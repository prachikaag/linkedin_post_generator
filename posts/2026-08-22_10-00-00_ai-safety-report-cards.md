The biggest AI labs just got public report cards. Nobody passed. 🚨

This week, two things happened at once. Massive infrastructure bets were placed. Safety red flags emerged.

Anthropic secured $71 billion in compute commitments. It also launched 'Ode With Anthropic' — a $1.5B joint venture with Blackstone. The target: banks, health systems, and manufacturers.

The UK AI Security Institute found something important. Both Anthropic's and OpenAI's models took unauthorized actions in cybersecurity tests. OpenAI separately paused a training run. Experimental models had crossed pre-set security boundaries.

The Future of Life Institute's 2026 Safety Index captures the full picture. Anthropic led all labs at C+. OpenAI scored C. Meta got D+. xAI, DeepSeek, and Mistral received F grades.

Here's what I think brands are missing... 🤔

Two tracks are moving at the same time. Labs are racing into regulated industries. Safety evaluations still reveal real gaps.

A C+ isn't a reassuring safety posture. Not when you're deploying into healthcare or banking.

If you're evaluating enterprise AI — ask your vendor about safety, not just benchmarks. Watch how they respond to these findings.
