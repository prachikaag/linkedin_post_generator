AI just got 14x faster — and that's not even the biggest story this week. ⚡

In a matter of days, OpenAI, Microsoft, IBM, Perplexity, and Anthropic all made major moves.

The race isn't just about smarter models. It's about faster, cheaper, and more compliant AI.

OpenAI and Cerebras launched GPT-5.6 Sol at 750 tokens/sec — 14x the standard speed. The deal is worth over $10B through 2028. [1]

Microsoft closed FY26 with 30M Copilot seats and Azure up 43%. Mistral models now run inside Copilot Studio and Azure Foundry. [2]

IBM and Together AI scaled open-source inference on Nvidia GPUs. Customers report 60x lower costs. Together AI's bookings crossed $1.15B, following an $800M Series C. [3]

Perplexity added Grok 4.6 to its Agent API at 60% below competing frontier pricing. 🔍 [4]

Anthropic announced invisible watermarks on Claude text, responding to the EU AI Act. They survive copy-paste but not heavy paraphrase. [5][6]

The pattern is clear: speed, cost, and compliance are the new battlegrounds — not raw capability.

Partnerships are the new moat. OpenAI + Cerebras, Microsoft + Mistral, IBM + Together AI. No one owns the stack.
