The AI funding machine just had one of its biggest weeks of the year. 💰

Two deals. Different sizes. Same signal.

Fal — a generative media inference startup — raised $1.3 billion at a $16 billion valuation this week.
Bessemer Venture Partners led. Nvidia joined as a strategic backer.

Traversal, which automates incident response using autonomous AI agents, just became a unicorn.
$120 million Series B. Kleiner Perkins, Redpoint, and Khosla co-led. Sequoia and Battery also joined.

Nvidia's participation in the fal round matters. 🧠
It's not a passive financial bet. It's a directional signal about where inference demand is heading.
Nvidia doesn't just follow deals — it backs the layer it needs to grow.

Meanwhile: approximately 2.5% of AI deals captured roughly 65% of venture dollars this month.
Capital is concentrating. Not spreading.

Here's what I think brands are missing...

These aren't just startup funding stories. They're bets on the two layers that power everything brands build on AI. ⚡
Inference: the speed layer. Agents: the decision layer.
Both are getting disproportionate capital right now.

The pick-and-shovel companies funded in late 2026 will define what's possible in your stack in 18 months.
Watch who Nvidia backs. Watch which agent infrastructure becomes standard.

Which AI infrastructure layer matters most to your team right now — inference speed or agent reliability?
