The AI race just went public.

Anthropic filed its S-1 on October 26. Nasdaq ticker: ANT. First-half revenue: $16.3 billion. This is no longer a startup story.

The IPO wasn't the only big move this week. 🏁

OpenAI launched GPT-6.1 Terra on the same day. It's built for computer use and visual reasoning. Priced at half the cost of GPT-5.6 Terra.

Anthropic also introduced Claude Duet — a proactive agent built on Opus 5.5. It acts without waiting to be asked. That's a different kind of AI.

The numbers in Anthropic's S-1 are worth pausing on. 📊

Five customers account for roughly a quarter of their first nine months of revenue. $16.3 billion in half-year revenue, yet an $80.75 billion net loss over the same period.

OpenAI cut input token pricing in half with GPT-6.1 Terra. The model race is also a price war.

What matters isn't which company is "winning." 💡

It's what this week reveals. IPOs. Price cuts. Proactive agents. All at once. This is a maturing market moving very fast.

For brands: the platform decisions you make now have real lock-in consequences. The cost of entry is falling — but the cost of switching is rising.

Which platform is your team building on — and was that choice deliberate?
