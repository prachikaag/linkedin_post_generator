The world's top two AI companies just became Apple's biggest hardware customers. 🤖

OpenAI bought tens of thousands of Mac minis and Mac Studios. Anthropic is renting them through AWS.

Both are training computer-use agents. These are AI systems that operate software the way a person would.

This isn't a chip story. It's about a fundamentally different kind of AI being built right now.

Apple's unified memory lets these companies run thousands of isolated agent sessions in parallel. GPU clusters are built for model training — not for this.

McKinsey 2026: organisations scaling AI agents rose from 27% to 40% in one year. The momentum is real.

Clay — the AI sales and marketing platform — just raised at a $7B valuation. Up from $5B eight months ago. Agentic AI workflows are where capital flows.

Google's Gemini 3.7 Flash launched recently as an agent-first model. Anthropic locked in Claude Sonnet 5 at $2 per million input tokens — permanently. The economics now support always-on agents.

The Mac mini story isn't Apple winning AI. It's a signal about what kind of AI is being built.

Computer-use agents don't need the single fastest machine. They need quantity — many machines, many sessions, learning by doing.

The physical layer of agentic AI is being quietly assembled while everyone debates benchmarks.

If agents can operate your tools the way your team does — what changes for your workflows? Not in five years. Right now.

The hardware is being purchased today.

What's the first repetitive task in your business you'd hand to a computer-use agent?
