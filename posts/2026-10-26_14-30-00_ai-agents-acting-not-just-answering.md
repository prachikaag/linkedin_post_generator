Every major AI platform launched autonomous agents this October.
That's not a coincidence — that's a convergence.

Anthropic launched Claude Duet, a proactive personal agent. 🤖
It runs background tasks and seeks approval before sensitive actions.
OpenAI introduced 'dots' — always-on agents living inside Slack and Teams.

Google launched agentic Gemini for enterprise on October 8. 📊
It can delegate tasks to subagents with its own workplace email identity.
Microsoft's Copilot Autopilot now operates the same way.
The FTC opened an investigation into AI-agent risks — watching Anthropic, OpenAI, and others.

Here's what I think brands are missing. 💡
This isn't just a feature update. It's a structural shift in how software works.
AI agents don't wait to be asked. They act, trigger workflows, and send messages.
The accountability gap is real: who is responsible when an agent makes a mistake?

Marketing and brand teams need approval workflows designed before deployment — not after.
Start small: one bounded task, clear guardrails, one owner.
Ask who signs off before the agent runs.

Are you already working with autonomous agents? What governance have you put in place?
