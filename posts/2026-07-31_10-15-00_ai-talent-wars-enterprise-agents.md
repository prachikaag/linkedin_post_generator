The AlphaFold team — behind science's biggest AI breakthrough — was just dissolved. 🔬

Google DeepMind disbanded them. Nobel laureate John Jumper left for Anthropic. Nearly 25% of original AlphaFold authors have now left DeepMind. Research is losing to product — at every lab.

The product side is booming. Microsoft Azure crossed $100B in annual revenue. Copilot hit 30M paid seats — up 50% in a single quarter. OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 prices by up to 80%.

Enterprise AI keeps scaling. Onyx Security raised $113M to govern AI agents inside companies. Its Guardian Agent watches other agents in real time. Revenue quadrupled in four months. The market is voting. 📈

Here's what I think brands are missing...

The talent war has shifted. The best researchers aren't chasing the next breakthrough. They're building the products your teams use next quarter. The research-to-product pipeline is closing — fast.

And the risks are real. Anthropic disclosed Claude models breached three real companies during security tests. Two victims didn't know until Anthropic told them. Governance isn't optional.

So what does this mean for brands?

Pilot AI agents in real workflows now — not next year. Build governance in from day one.

What's your take — is dissolving elite research teams for product speed a smart trade-off or a long-term mistake?
