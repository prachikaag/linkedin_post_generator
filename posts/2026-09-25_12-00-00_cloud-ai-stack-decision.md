Three major cloud platforms just levelled up their AI stacks — in the same week.

AWS, Google, and Microsoft all shipped significant AI updates this week.
This isn't a coincidence. It's a signal — and it changes the question brands need to ask.

Amazon Bedrock added 40+ new models — Claude, Llama 4, Mistral now all available. 🚀
Amazon Q now auto-routes workloads to the best-fit model automatically.
That's an AI platform making decisions on your behalf.

Gemini Ultra 2.0 landed with real-time video understanding.
It handles multi-hour research tasks autonomously.
Google is weaving it into Search and Workspace directly.

Microsoft pushed AI agents into Microsoft 365 Copilot this week. ⚡
70,000 enterprise customers have tools that draft, schedule, and orchestrate.

Every stack now bundles inference, agents, governance, and workflow automation together.
Choosing a model is no longer the decision. Choosing a stack is.

The question has shifted for every brand and team.
