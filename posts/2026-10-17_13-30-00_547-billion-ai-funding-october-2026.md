$5.47 billion. 41 funding rounds. 16 days. That's October 2026 for AI. 🏗️

The capital isn't slowing — it's concentrating. Defense, legal, frontier models, and enterprise tools are where the biggest checks are landing right now.

Shield AI closed a $1.5 billion Series G at a $12.7 billion valuation. 💰 Legora, a legal AI company, raised $550 million at a $5.55 billion valuation. Butterfly Effect, an AI model company, pulled in $500 million in Series C. Arena raised $200 million to build frontier models and agents at a $3.1 billion valuation.

These aren't bets on a category. They're bets on specific problems. 🔍 Legal AI at $5.55B says investors believe AI can handle high-stakes professional work reliably. Defense AI at $12.7B says the same about critical systems.

The pattern: the biggest checks go to domains where ROI is measurable and the accuracy bar is high. Finance, legal, healthcare, defense. Categories where a 5% improvement pays for itself in months.

For brand teams watching this: ✨ the money flow tells you where the next generation of enterprise tools is being built. Vertical AI — built for one industry's specific language, risk, and workflow — is where the serious capital is going right now.
