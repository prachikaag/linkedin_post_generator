The AI model race just went fully open. 🌐

Three companies are shipping flagship models in parallel right now. The pace is unlike anything we've seen before.

Mistral just released open weights for Mistral Large 4 — a 1-trillion-parameter model. [1] It supports multimodal input and a 1M token context window. Full weights, publicly available for self-hosting.

That is not a small bet. It is a statement about where Mistral thinks the market is heading.

Meanwhile, Anthropic iterated twice on its Mythos-class architecture this year. [3][4] Claude Fable 5.1 improved on coding and cut cache costs — built for longer agentic runs.

Google confirmed Gemini 4 Argon is already in the hands of cybersecurity partners. [5] And new variants — Hydrogen and Krypton — are being teased for broader release before year-end. [6]

Here's what I think brands are missing...

Open weights change the economics entirely. When a 1-trillion-parameter model can run on your own infrastructure, the conversation shifts from "which API should we use?" to "what do we build?"

The real competition is no longer just at the API level. It is at the agent level — who builds the best tooling on top of these models.

Brands that want to stay competitive need to follow the model releases. Not to pick a winner — there isn't one yet. But to understand the capabilities they can access today.
