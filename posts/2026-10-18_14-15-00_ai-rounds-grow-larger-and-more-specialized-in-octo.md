The AI infrastructure buildout is the story most marketers are ignoring. 💰

While attention focuses on new model launches, the real transformation is happening elsewhere. It's in the power, compute, and capital flowing into AI infrastructure.

US AI data center startups raised $33.5 billion in 2026 alone. That's almost double the full-year 2025 total.

Nvidia and major Wall Street firms — Goldman Sachs, Apollo, BlackRock, KKR — are mobilising $500 billion for AI infrastructure. Bank of America launched its own $250 billion initiative focused on US data centers in August. The big four hyperscalers — Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft — are forecast to spend $650 billion on capital expenditure this year. 🏗️

AI funding rounds are growing more specialised this month. It's no longer general AI — it's infrastructure, security, and vertical applications.

Power is the main bottleneck, according to a Morgan Stanley report. The capital is moving to solve it.

Model releases get the headlines. Infrastructure is what makes deployment possible at scale.

When Wall Street banks are financing AI data centers, this is no longer a tech story. It's a capital markets story. The brands that understand this aren't asking "should we use AI?" They're asking "how do we design for the world this builds?" 🧠

Marketing and brand teams need to stop thinking about AI as a collection of tools. Start thinking about it as foundational infrastructure.

The services your team will depend on in 2027 are being built right now. Your vendors, platforms, and agencies are building on this foundation. Ask them how.

Is your organisation treating AI as a tactical tool or as foundational infrastructure — and what's the real difference in practice?
