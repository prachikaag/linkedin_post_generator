$38 billion. That's how much AI startups raised in November 2026 alone. 📈

This month isn't just big — it's structurally different from anything we've seen before.

It's not one company dominating headlines. The entire AI stack is getting funded at once.

OpenAI recently closed $11 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation. That's the headline.

Thinking Machines Lab raised $5 billion. Fal raised $1.3 billion for AI infrastructure.

Genesis AI, backed by Nvidia, raised $500 million at a $3.5 billion valuation. Mercor reached a $20 billion valuation.

Corma raised $125 million for AI-powered cybersecurity agents. And 48% of November's 371 startup rounds went to AI and machine learning.

Here's what I think brands are missing: this isn't just a foundation model story.

The money is flowing to agents, data pipelines, and security infrastructure. Enterprise AI agents are becoming real products — not demos. The tools your marketing team will actually use are being funded right now.

If you're waiting for AI to mature before building strategy — that's a risk. 🎯

The tools reshaping creative and marketing work are funded, in development, and arriving fast.
