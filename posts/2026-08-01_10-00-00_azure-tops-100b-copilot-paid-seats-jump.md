Azure just crossed $100 billion in annual revenue. 🚀
That number would have been unthinkable three years ago.

The AI infrastructure race accelerated on every front this week.
Microsoft, Nvidia, and Perplexity all made significant moves.
The gap between early adopters and everyone else is widening.

Microsoft's Q4 numbers were striking — Azure grew 43% and Copilot hit 30 million paid seats. [1]
Seat adds more than doubled quarter over quarter.
Microsoft announced a unified app merging Copilot, GitHub Copilot, and AI agents into one interface. [2]

Jensen Huang said the chip industry must grow tenfold to power 100 billion agents. [3]
Perplexity also launched a Windows agent routing tasks across 20 models. [4]
And Microsoft unveiled its first cybersecurity AI model, scoring 96% on CyberGym. [5]

This isn't just a Microsoft story — it's a consolidation story. 🧩
Every major player is betting agents will become the default work interface.
The unified Copilot app tells you everything.
When chat, code, and agents merge into one tool, adoption friction disappears.
That's when AI stops being a tool and starts being infrastructure.

Brands still in pilot mode are falling behind.
The enterprise AI stack is consolidating — fewer decisions, but higher stakes.
