Every major AI company just watched DeepSeek do it again. 👁️

DeepSeek V4 launched as open source. 16 trillion parameter mixture-of-experts architecture.
1 million token context window. Apache 2.0 licence.
Already on the API. Already free to use commercially.

The V4 family has two variants: a Flash model at 284B parameters and a Pro model at 1.6T parameters.
Both come with no commercial licensing fees. That's the part that matters. 🔓

Third-party benchmarks put Kimi K3 ahead on some reasoning scores.
DeepSeek's strength has never been benchmark-topping. It's been price-performance ratio.
And on that measure, it keeps compressing the gap.

The pattern here matters more than the model. 📉

Every open-source release from DeepSeek tightens the floor on what closed API pricing can charge.
The AI tools your marketing team uses today were built on a cost structure from 2024–2025.
That cost structure is shifting beneath them.

This isn't a niche developer story. It's a commercial infrastructure story.

If your team is building any AI-powered workflow — now is a good time to audit the model costs underneath it.
Open-source parity with frontier models changes the build-vs-buy calculation. Worth revisiting.
