Three creative AI tools shipped major updates this past week. 🎨

Midjourney v7 gained agentic capabilities and launched creator office hours.
Runway introduced a Model Router — it picks the best model for cost, speed, or quality.
ElevenLabs Music v2 now supports expressive audio tags: [laughs], [whispers], and more.
Their Eleven v3 voice is also now live inside Runway's pipeline.

The ElevenLabs tags are what I want to test first.
Adding emotional cues to audio — real human texture — changes what's possible in brand content.

The Runway Model Router is quietly significant.
Letting the system route by your constraints means less manual trial-and-error.
That's real time back in your creative workflow.

One to watch: Runway ends its Unlimited plan on September 1.
Everyone transitions to the new Max plan.
If your team is on Unlimited, review your usage now.

For brand teams, Eleven v3 inside Runway puts voice and video in one pipeline. 🔊
The expressive tags enable human-sounding brand audio — ads, podcasts, voice content — without a studio.
