The AI infrastructure stack just got a major overhaul. 🔧

The Model Context Protocol released its biggest spec update since launch. MCP 2026-07-28 replaces its stateful protocol with a stateless core. That enables serverless and edge AI agent deployments.

Three new official extensions arrive: MCP Apps, Tasks, and Enterprise Managed Auth. MCP now hits 400 million monthly SDK downloads — a 4x jump this year. [1][2]

Claude Opus 5 also landed this week, scoring 96% on SWE-bench Verified. It scores roughly 20 times higher than Opus 4.8 on ARC-AGI-3. It costs $5 input and $25 output per million tokens. [3]
