$180 million. $1.8 billion valuation. One third of the Fortune 100 as customers.
Profound just raised a Series D co-led by Sequoia and Kleiner Perkins. And the problem it's solving matters for every brand team.

Profound builds an agent it calls AI Marketer. 🤖
It analyzes where your brand appears in AI answers — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity — and deploys sub-agents to improve that presence through content, earned media, and paid campaigns.
Comcast, Estée Lauder, Walmart, and Royal Bank of Canada are already using it.

That timing is not a coincidence.

This week also saw Optimizely release purpose-built AI models for enterprise marketing — predicting content performance and personalizing experiences in real time.
Adobe acquired Rilo to strengthen AI workflow orchestration.
Meta AI now connects ad campaigns directly into Google Workspace. 📣
The stack for AI-native marketing is assembling itself fast.

Here's what I keep coming back to: the brands winning in AI search right now are the ones who started paying attention to it six months ago.
AI answers are replacing the first page of Google for a growing slice of search queries.
If you don't know whether your brand shows up in those answers — or how — you're operating blind.

Profound's raise is a signal. Not hype — capital. Sequoia and Kleiner don't write $180M checks into vague categories.

For brand and marketing leaders: AI search presence is the new SEO. ✨
The measurement tools exist. The question is whether your team is using them.

Is your brand optimizing for AI search — and if so, what's working?
