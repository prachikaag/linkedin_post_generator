The AI funding machine just hit a new gear. 🚀

Recently, Fireworks AI raised $1.5 billion in a Series D. Together AI raised $800 million. Both now report over $1 billion in annual bookings. That's not speculation — that's enterprise conviction in action.

And it's not just infrastructure getting funded. Anthropic recently launched Claude Opus 5. It outperforms competitors — at half the price. The AI cost per intelligence is in freefall. The AI model release pace in 2026 is relentless. 📉

Enterprises are ditching closed, expensive models for open-source alternatives. The inference layer — the plumbing that runs AI at scale — is now serious business. That's where the capital is concentrating.

Here's what I think brands are missing...

This funding wave means your AI tools will get faster and cheaper. More capable, too — whether you're ready or not.

Brands building flexible workflows today will be first to use new capabilities as they drop. Those waiting for the right moment? They'll spend next year catching up.

Map which tools in your marketing stack rely on inference infrastructure. Build workflows that can swap models easily. Speed and cost advantages will trickle down — sooner than most expect. 🧭
