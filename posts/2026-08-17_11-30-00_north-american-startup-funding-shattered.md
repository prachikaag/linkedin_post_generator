$407 billion flowed to AI startups in just the first half of 2026. 🤯
That's more than all of 2025 combined.

This isn't just big numbers. Something structural is happening.
Investors aren't just backing frontier labs anymore.
They're betting on the layer between frontier models and real business use.

Safe Superintelligence raised $5 billion — no product, no revenue.
Atoms, Travis Kalanick's physical AI startup, pulled in $1.7 billion from a16z.
Fireworks AI closed a $1.505 billion Series D.
It builds tools to customise general models on enterprise data.
Together AI raised $800 million, backed by Aramco Ventures, Nvidia, and Salesforce Ventures.
OpenAI and Anthropic alone absorbed 43% of every global VC dollar in H1 2026.

Here's what I think brands are missing...
The real money isn't just flowing to model makers.
It's going to the customisation layer — inference, fine-tuning, specialisation.
Fireworks AI's $17.5B valuation says the future isn't just using ChatGPT.
It's building your own version, trained on your proprietary data.

The brands that win won't just prompt generic models.
They'll build purpose-built systems on their own knowledge and workflows.
That infrastructure is being funded right now. Your strategy should catch up.

Where are you on this — still using off-the-shelf AI, or starting to customise?
