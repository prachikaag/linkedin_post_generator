The AI economy now has two completely different frontiers. ⚡

At one end: OpenAI raising $11B at a $1.25 trillion valuation.
At the other end, MountAIn raised €7.3M to run advanced AI on chips costing under $10.
Both happened this month. Both matter for where AI is heading.

The numbers tell the full picture.
OpenAI's round attracted SoftBank, MGX, Coatue, and Andreessen Horowitz.
It was scaled back from an earlier $30B target — still the year's largest private AI round.
AMD completed its World Labs acquisition this week, adding Fei-Fei Li as chief scientist.
Claude Fable 5.2 launched with API pricing 20% lower than its predecessor. 📉
The cost of frontier AI capability is falling even as valuations climb.

Here is what I think brands are missing.
When inference approaches commodity pricing, the competitive edge shifts.
It moves from who has access to who knows how to use it.
Brand teams building AI fluency now will own the next wave.

Stop benchmarking against billion-dollar companies. 🎯
The question is not whether you can afford AI tools.
It is whether your team is ready to build with them.

What would your team build if AI inference cost a fraction of today's price?
