Six AI platforms updated their core products this week. For brand and creative teams, the creative stack just got richer.

Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5 for demanding, long-horizon reasoning work. Google released Gemini 3.7 Flash — its most capable coding and agentic model yet. OpenAI made GPT-5.6 Luna the new default for free ChatGPT users.

The creative tools are catching up fast. 🎨

ElevenLabs released Music v2 with major quality improvements for AI-generated audio. Actor Mode gives creators fine control over voice — pauses, accents, intonation. Audiobook publishing to Spotify is now live. Runway's video generation now works inside major AI assistant interfaces. Midjourney's summer updates bring faster generation and better style consistency.

The real pattern here isn't new models — it's integration. AI tools are embedding into the platforms where work already happens. Claude Fable 5's long-horizon reasoning suits complex brand strategy tasks. Gemini 3.7 Flash coding agents now serve product teams directly.

Here's what I see brands missing: the creative layer is maturing fast. Voice, video, image, and text — all upgrading at the same time. Teams that wait for "the right moment" will spend next year catching up. 💡
