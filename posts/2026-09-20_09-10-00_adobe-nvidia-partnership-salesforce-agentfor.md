Salesforce just renamed its Marketing Cloud. Not a rebrand. A platform bet. 🔄

The new name is Agentforce Marketing.
The premise: marketers work alongside AI agents that generate briefs, content, and full customer journeys — and optimize spend — all from a prompt.

This wasn't the only signal this week. 📣
Adobe announced a strategic partnership with NVIDIA to rebuild the next generation of Firefly models and enterprise-grade agentic workflows.
Adobe Sensei running on NVIDIA NIM microservices.
Jensen Huang called it "foundational for creative industry transformation" — that's infrastructure language, not feature language.

What strikes me about both moves: neither company bolted AI onto what already existed.
Both restructured their platforms around agents as the primary interface.
Not dashboards. Not wizard flows. Agents.

Here's the skill shift nobody is naming clearly enough. ✨
The question for marketing teams is no longer "how do I use this tool."
It's "how do I prompt, review, and direct AI agents doing this work at scale."
Human-in-the-loop isn't optional — it's the product design.

The creative stack marketers use every day is being rebuilt this year.
Teams getting ahead aren't waiting for the platform to ship a training module.
They're running experiments on what works now — and building that muscle early.

Has AI started changing your creative or marketing workflow — or are you still waiting for the right moment to start?
