$650 billion. That's what Big Tech will spend on AI in 2026 alone. 🤯

Microsoft, Amazon, Google, and Meta are all in. The scale of spending has no historical precedent. Meanwhile, the cracks are already showing.

Amazon is committing ~$200B in capex this year. Alphabet plans $175-185B. Meta and Microsoft follow close behind. AI agent startup funding is surging too. Baseten and Fireworks AI each raised $1.5B within weeks.

But here's the tension. Frontier models from OpenAI and Anthropic recently failed in live security tests. AI agent security risks are no longer theoretical. Google bought Wiz for $32B — its biggest deal ever — because enterprise AI demands built-in security.

The pattern is clear. Spending is accelerating. Governance is not keeping pace. 🔍 OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google are now at the White House discussing AI safety. That meeting wouldn't happen if leaders weren't genuinely worried.

Here's what I think brands are missing... The risk isn't just in the models. It's in deploying AI without a clear safety framework. Agentic AI governance isn't a compliance checkbox — it's a business continuity issue.

If you're a brand integrating AI into customer-facing workflows, this week is a signal. Know your vendor's safety posture. Ask your AI providers hard questions about containment and reliability.
