Competitor analysis. 5 brands. 20 minutes instead of 3 hours.

I ran a simple test. Perplexity Pro versus my own desk research.
The brief: summarise positioning, recent campaigns, and tone of voice for 5 competitors.
No hints. Same task I'd give a junior strategist.

4 out of 5 brand summaries were accurate. Solid positioning reads. Useful tone analysis.
The fifth had recently rebranded. Perplexity missed it entirely. Old narrative. Full confidence.
But it cited every source — so fact-checking was fast, not skipped.

Manual research: 3 hours. Perplexity: 20 minutes.

That's not a small time saving. That's a full half-day back.

Here's what it couldn't do: synthesis.

Perplexity gave me information — not insight.
The strategic read still needed me. What the data means. Where the gaps are.
That part isn't going anywhere.

What it IS: a data-gathering accelerator.
Every strategist who uses it properly gets 2–3 hours back — per project.

For brand teams: stop rebuilding competitor research from scratch each time.
Use Perplexity for the retrieval phase. Reserve your strategist's time for the analysis.

Have you used Perplexity for competitive research? What worked and what didn't?
