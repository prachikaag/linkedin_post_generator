$3.3 billion raised in one week. AI funding isn't slowing — it's accelerating. 🚀

November 2026 opened with a signal hard to ignore.

Fal, a generative media platform, raised $1.3B in a Series F.
Led by Bessemer Venture Partners, at a $16B valuation.
That follows a $140M raise just 11 months ago. The pace is extraordinary.

The data points stack up fast. 💰

Fal's combined $1.6B this month ranks among the year's largest AI funding events.

Bessemer's thesis: fal owns real-time generative media infrastructure — the layer powering AI content at scale.

Sequoia Capital raised a $7B expansion fund under new co-stewards Alfred Lin and Pat Grady.
Their focus: early and growth-stage AI bets.
Sequoia appeared in two separate rounds this month alone.

Here's what I think brands are missing...

The gap between private AI valuations and public markets is widening fast.

Fal went from $140M to a $16B valuation in under a year. Generative media infrastructure is the new battleground.

Meanwhile, JPMorgan warns of an AI stocks correction. Nvidia expectations are running hot.
The smart money and the equity analysts disagree — for now.

For brands, this matters more than it looks.

The tools you use today likely run on infrastructure fal is building. If this layer consolidates fast, your creative stack changes — plan for it now.

Where do you think AI funding goes next — consolidation, or new breakout categories?
