AI agents are no longer a future concept. They're shipping — fast. 🚀

October and November 2026 have been relentless. Google, SAP, Anthropic, OpenAI — all pushing agents into enterprise workflows at once.

This isn't one wave. It's a flood.

Google's Gemini Enterprise agent lets you set objectives, not instructions. It spawns sub-agents and routes work to other models — including Anthropic's Claude. (MediaPost, Oct 2026)

SAP expanded Joule with multi-step workflow planning and governance features. (AI Agent Store)

Anthropic opened a Message Threads beta — persistent multi-turn agent conversations. (Agentic.ai)

October alone saw 22 new LLM releases from 17 providers. GPT-6.1 Terra. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash Cyber. Kimi K3.1 Pro. (LLM Gateway)

Here's what I think brands are missing...

The real race isn't which model is best. It's how to orchestrate many agents without losing control.

Multi-agent orchestration is becoming a core enterprise discipline. BetterCloud is already selling AI agent lifecycle management to IT teams. When SaaS vendors package governance tooling, adoption is mainstream. (VidAU.ai)

If you're deploying AI agents — even one — you need a governance layer now.

Who owns each agent? What can it access? Who reviews its outputs? These aren't just IT questions. They're brand and risk questions.

Is your team tracking which AI agents are running across your business? Or is it still the Wild West?
