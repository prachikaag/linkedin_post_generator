ChatGPT just became an advertising platform. 🚨

OpenAI launched Sponsored AI Agents — click an ad and start a brand conversation. Not a landing page. A live conversation with an AI.

This isn't a one-off. Every major platform is racing to own the marketing workflow.

94% of marketers now use AI in content creation, per HubSpot. Apple just added an AI writing assistant to Keynote, Pages, and Numbers. It's going head-to-head with Microsoft Copilot. Anthropic launched Claude Duet to automate professional workflows. The platforms are consolidating fast.

OpenAI's Sponsored AI Agents are in beta with select US advertisers. Brands pay for a conversation, not a click.

Apple's new tools support Model Context Protocol. AI agents now connect directly to your creative stack. Microsoft is splitting Copilot into usage-based billing from December 2026.

Here's what I think brands are missing...

The AI platforms ARE the new ad platforms. Google and Meta built empires on search discovery. The next wave is AI discovery. If your brand isn't shaping what AI says about you, you're already behind. 🎯

Two things to act on now:

1. Ask ChatGPT, Claude, and Apple Intelligence what they say about your brand. That's your AI shelf space.
2. Start exploring conversational ad formats — not just static display.

Are you thinking about AI discoverability yet — or is this still a future problem?
