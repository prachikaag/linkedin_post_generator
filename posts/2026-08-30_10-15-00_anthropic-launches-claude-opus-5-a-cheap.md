The price of frontier AI was just cut in half. 💡

That's the most significant business development in AI right now.

Claude Opus 5 delivers near-frontier performance at half the cost of Anthropic's Fable 5. It scored 42/42 on the 2026 International Math Olympiad. It outperformed the previous Opus on agentic tasks by more than 15 percentage points.

And it's not alone. GPT-5.6 dropped pricing by up to 80%. Gemini 3.7 Flash runs at $0.75 per million input tokens.

The intelligence commodity cycle is here.

Meanwhile, Google restructured DeepMind to compete harder. 🔄 Demis Hassabis stepped back to become chair. Koray Kavukcuoglu now runs day-to-day model development. Jeff Dean — 27 years at Google — left to found a new AI research company.

Amazon shut down Mechanical Turk after 21 years. It trained a generation of AI models. Then AI made it obsolete.

Here's what I think this all adds up to:

AI capability is becoming a commodity. The cost floor drops while the ceiling rises. The race is no longer about who has the best model. It's about who owns the application and integration layer. 🎯

For marketing and brand teams:

There is no longer a budget reason to avoid frontier AI. The question has shifted from access to integration and workflow design.

What's still stopping your team from fully adopting AI in daily work?
