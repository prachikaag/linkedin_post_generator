Something shifted in the model landscape this October. 🌊

In less than two weeks, three major AI companies released new models. They didn't overlap — they scattered across the market.

Meta launched Muse Spark Open on October 15. It's an 816-billion-parameter model. You can run it on paired Mac Desktops.

Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5 on October 7. It costs 75% less to run than the previous version. On agentic benchmarks, it jumped from 15.7% to 72.4%.

DeepSeek pushed out V4.1-Flash-1017 on October 17. Faster. Leaner. Better benchmarks — and openly available.

The pattern here isn't competition. It's distribution. 🔍

Open-weight models are moving closer to enterprise-quality performance. The cost floor is dropping. An AI agent needing a frontier API budget six months ago now runs locally.

This changes the calculus for brand teams. 🏗

Custom models aren't just for tech companies anymore. With engineering support, teams can fine-tune Muse Spark Open on brand data. No API fees. Your data stays on your servers.

The cheapest capable model is now genuinely cheap. That means AI in the workflow, not just in the strategy deck.
