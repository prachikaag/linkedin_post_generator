AI model costs just dropped dramatically. The question is: are you ready to act on it? 📊

Mistral, Anthropic, and OpenAI all made significant moves this month. The common thread is cost reduction and accessibility. Picking the right model for the right task is now genuinely possible.

Mistral Large 4 entered public preview recently with a 1-million-token context window. [1] It scored 61.7% on coding benchmarks. Open weights are due by end of October, including a self-hosted option for brands.

GPT-6 Luna rolled out to all free ChatGPT users recently. [2] At $0.10 per million tokens, it costs less than most SaaS tools per month. GPT-6 Sol, for paid users, handles complex agent tasks at $2 per million tokens.

Anthropic launched Claude Duet, its first proactive background agent, for Pro subscribers. [3] EU regulators activated new AI enforcement powers. Fines can reach 3% of global turnover for non-compliance. [4]

Here is the shift that matters: frontier-quality, open-weight models are arriving. 🔍

Mistral Large 4 beat Kimi K3 in independent evaluations. GPT-6 Luna undercuts most tools on cost. You don't need the most expensive model. You need the right model for the right task.

For marketing and brand teams, now is the time to audit your AI stack. Map which tasks need frontier capability. Find which just need fast and affordable.
