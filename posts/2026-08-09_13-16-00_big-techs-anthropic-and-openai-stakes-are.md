$33 billion raised. In seven months. Already more than all of 2025. 📊

AI investment hit a record this year — and the concentration is extraordinary. Two companies captured 43% of all capital raised. 88% of it flowed to U.S.-based operations. Meanwhile, Big Tech's balance sheets are quietly entangled with the companies they theoretically compete against.

Microsoft reported a $480 million gain on its OpenAI stake last quarter — while building competing models. 🤯
Amazon and Alphabet are in similar territory with Anthropic. The lines between investor, partner, and competitor are blurring in ways the market has never seen before.

The startup layer is moving fast too. Atoms — Travis Kalanick's physical AI startup — raised $1.7 billion, led by Andreessen Horowitz. Fireworks AI closed a $1.5 billion Series D. Together AI secured $800 million in Series C funding. Anthropic is now building its own chip team, signalling a deeper infrastructure bet.

Here's what I think brands are missing...

Investors are tightening the criteria. They want paying users, narrow workflows, protected data rights, and a repeatable sales path. Not just a compelling demo.

That's the same standard your team should be applying internally to every AI initiative. The companies getting funded are building for specific, measurable outcomes. That's the bar now.

What outcome would you need to see to double your AI investment this year?
