China blocked Meta from buying it. Meta had to hand it back.

Then Manus raised $500 million anyway. 💰

Butterfly Effect — Manus's parent company — closed the round on October 8. Boyu Capital and IDG Capital led. Tencent also invested.

The company's annualised revenue had already hit $500 million by June 2026. That's the number that matters here.

The geopolitics forced Meta's exit. The business kept growing regardless.

Manus is targeting a $4 billion valuation. Its first funding round since Beijing forced the unwind — and it didn't need Meta to get there.

Same week: Nous Research raised $90 million at a $1.5 billion valuation. 🌱

Their open-source Hermes model has been cloned 24 million times. Revenue is tracking toward $100 million by year-end. Investors include Nvidia and Samsung.

AI startups have raised $74.1 billion across 77 rounds in 2026 so far.

Here's what I keep thinking about for brands...

The tools getting funded right now aren't speculative. They're production-ready AI agents replacing workflows — not supplementing them.

Manus builds computer-use agents. Nous builds open-source enterprise agents. Both are growing revenue fast.

The funding story tells you where the market is heading. Not hype cycles — actual enterprise spend.

For any brand leader watching this space: your vendors are already building on these platforms. Whether you know it or not.
