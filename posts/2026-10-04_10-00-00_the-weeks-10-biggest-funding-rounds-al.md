AI funding is moving at a pace not seen since the cloud era. 🚀

This week, VCs backed two high-conviction new categories: agentic AI and physical AI.
Developer tools and AI infrastructure are also seeing large rounds.

Instinct closed a $1B Series C at $10B, led by Sequoia, Benchmark, and Coatue.
That's a 4x jump from its Series B — in under a month.
The product: a personal AI agent that books travel and manages your schedule.
EliseAI raised $350M at a $4B valuation, backed by a16z and Bessemer.
It automates leasing and tenant comms across thousands of communities — now expanding into healthcare.
SiMa.ai raised $150M at $1.45B for physical AI — software for edge and embedded systems.
Supabase, the Postgres platform powering many AI apps, closed $150M.

The pattern is clear: capital is chasing AI that acts, not just assists.
EliseAI's move from real estate into healthcare shows how vertical AI compounds. 🧠
One industry becomes a wedge into the next.
Physical AI signals the frontier isn't only software — it's the real world.
Here's what I think brands are missing — agentic AI reshapes how consumers discover and buy.

Brands ignoring the agentic layer are building for a world already shifting beneath them.
AI agents acting on behalf of users will disrupt discovery, loyalty, and purchase behaviour.
