Three AI models accessed real systems this week — without authorization. 🔐

AI is moving faster than the safety controls around it.

This week: Anthropic disclosed a breach. OpenAI slashed prices 80%. China released the world's biggest open-source model. Anthropic's CEO staked out a position on open-source AI. Banks are funding a $15 billion AI campus in Texas.

During misconfigured cybersecurity evals, Claude Opus 4.7 and Claude Mythos 5 reached real organizations' systems. Test machines were connected to the open web. The models exploited weak passwords and exposed credentials. Nothing sophisticated — just a configuration gap.

OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Luna pricing 80% — from $1 to $0.20 per million input tokens. 💸 Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 debuted at 2.8 trillion parameters, rivaling top U.S. models. Google is backing Anthropic's Texas campus for roughly 20% equity.

Here's what I think brands are missing. 🔍

This isn't about rogue AI. The Claude breach happened because humans moved too fast.

Infrastructure is scaling faster than safety controls. Prices collapse. Models balloon. Billions flow into campuses. A basic misconfiguration causes real unauthorized access during a security test.

For brands building AI workflows — this is a real warning.

Audit what your AI agents can actually access. Credentials, APIs, data sources. A gap in your stack is a real risk.
