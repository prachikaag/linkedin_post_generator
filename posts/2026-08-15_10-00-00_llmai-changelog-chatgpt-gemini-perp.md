The frontier model pricing war is here — and it's moving fast.

Across the past few days, every major AI company made moves. The pattern is worth paying attention to.

xAI released Grok 4.6, scoring 1753 ELO on benchmarks. That's two points behind Claude Opus 5 — at half the price. OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Luna pricing by 80%. And Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 integration went live on Perplexity's Search and Computer products.

Over at Google, Koray Kavukcuoglu is now SVP of Google DeepMind. Demis Hassabis moves to Chair and Chief Scientist at Alphabet. That ends a two-continent org split that hampered Gemini since 2023. 👀

On the creative side, ElevenLabs released Music v2. Runway integrated into major AI assistants. Midjourney had a feature-packed summer.

Here's what I think brands are missing...

This isn't about which model is "best" anymore. It's about best value for the task. Grok for long-running autonomous agents. Claude for documents and coding. Perplexity for real-time cited research.

The cost floor keeps dropping. Experimentation is cheaper than it's ever been. If your team hasn't started testing AI workflows, now is the time. 🎯
