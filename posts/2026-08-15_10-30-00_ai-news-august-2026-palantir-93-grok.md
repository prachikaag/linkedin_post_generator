The frontier AI pricing war just got very interesting. 🔥

The AI market is reshuffling on multiple fronts.

Google replaced Demis Hassabis at DeepMind with Koray Kavukcuoglu. xAI launched Grok 4.6 at half the cost of rival frontier models. And OpenAI recently cut GPT-5.6 Luna pricing by 80% — while serving one billion weekly users.

These moves share a common signal: frontier model pricing is falling fast.

Grok 4.6 scores 1753 ELO on competitive benchmarks. It matches GPT-5.6 Sol from OpenAI on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. Pricing holds at $2/$6 per million tokens — half comparable models. xAI built it specifically for long-running autonomous agent tasks. 🤖

Meanwhile, Google is openly playing catch-up. Internal struggles forced the DeepMind restructuring — stalled Gemini development, missed deadlines, a significant talent exodus. That context matters.

Here's what I think brands are missing...

This isn't just a benchmark race. It's a platform race. Pricing, ecosystem depth, and agent reliability are the real differentiators now.

If your team builds on AI APIs, falling prices are good news. But which platform will still be gaining momentum in 12 months? That's the harder question. 💡
