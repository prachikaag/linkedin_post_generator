A seed round. Then $870M. In weeks. 💰
That's not a funding round — that's a market calling its shot.

TypeSafe AI closed an $870M Series A this month at a $7.5B valuation. Weeks earlier, it had raised a $40M seed. a16z led, with Sequoia participating.

Swedish legal AI startup Legora closed a $550M Series E the same week. 🏛️
Valuation: $8.8B, backed by Atreides Management and JP Morgan. Legal infrastructure, at scale.

TypeSafe's model, Jev, makes calibrated decisions rather than generating text. ⚖️
That's not an AI assistant. That's a judgment engine. Institutional capital moved fast because it replaces professional analysis, not writing.

Legora tells the same story. Law firms and in-house teams aren't experimenting — they're choosing platforms.

What I notice isn't the dollar amounts. It's the category. 🔍
Decision-making AI. Legal AI. Both are about accuracy over speed. Both are invisible to most marketing strategies.

Brands building AI roadmaps around content and chatbots alone will miss this layer.

The biggest AI investment right now flows into judgment and compliance workflows. That's just above where most marketing teams look. It will reshape every agency, vendor, and enterprise relationship.
