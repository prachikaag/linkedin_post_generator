The AI model release pace 2026 is unlike anything we've seen. 🚀

Three major labs. Multiple new models. All within weeks of each other. The race isn't about capability alone — it's about access and cost.

Recently, OpenAI gave free ChatGPT users unlimited text conversations. Their new default model, GPT-5.6 Luna, cut factual errors by 62%. Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5. Same price as Opus 4.8 — but rivaling their top Fable model in performance. Google upgraded Search AI Mode with Gemini 3.7 Flash. Today, it also launched Ask Gemini in Google Chat.

The Claude Opus 5 rollout is the one I keep thinking about. Top-tier performance. Half the cost of Fable. That's AI cost per intelligence drop made real. It forces brands to rethink their entire AI stack. 💡

Here's what I think brands are missing: the bottleneck is no longer the model. It's knowing which model fits which job. Luna for scale. Sol for depth. Opus 5 for enterprise reasoning. Choosing wrong now has real cost implications.

Map your use cases before you commit to a platform. Models are converging. Informed buyers aren't.
