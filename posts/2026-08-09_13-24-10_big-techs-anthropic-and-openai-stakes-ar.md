Big Tech's earnings now partly reflect paper gains on AI bets.

The AI funding market is unlike anything seen before.
$33 billion has entered AI startups in 2026, already surpassing all of 2025.
Anthropic is even building its own chip team — no longer just a software play.

Microsoft booked a $480M gain on its OpenAI stake last quarter.
Amazon and Alphabet also booked significant AI investment gains last quarter.
Yet Microsoft is simultaneously building competing models against those same companies.
Two companies alone captured 43% of all AI funding raised this year. 💰
New rounds keep landing: Atoms raised $1.7B, Fireworks AI $1.5B, Together AI $800M.

The real story is not the size of these rounds. 🎯
It is the strange geometry of who is investing in whom.
Big Tech holds stakes in rivals and builds against them at the same time.
That is not just unusual — it reshapes market dynamics in unpredictable ways.
Investors are also shifting what they reward: proof, paying users, and clear revenue paths.

For brand and marketing leaders, this consolidation carries direct consequences.
When two companies hold nearly half of all AI capital, concentration risk is real.
Build flexibility into your AI choices now — before lock-in makes it harder.

Are you thinking about AI vendor concentration risk — or just chasing the best model available?
