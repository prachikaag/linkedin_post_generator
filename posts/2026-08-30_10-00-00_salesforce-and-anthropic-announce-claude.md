Claude just became the default AI engine inside Salesforce. 🔥

Anthropic and Salesforce announced Claudeforce this week. Claude is now embedded across Agentforce, Slack, and the Atlas Reasoning Engine. 37 prebuilt sales skills. Open beta arrives in September.

That's not a feature launch. That's infrastructure.

And Claude isn't stopping at sales software. 🤖

Anthropic recently released the Model Hardware Standard — a shared spec for AI agents to safely operate physical devices. Anthropic's MCP and Google's A2A now share the same governance roof at the Linux Foundation. AWS, Google, Microsoft, and OpenAI are all members.

The agent communication stack is being standardized right now.

Here's my read on the bigger picture:

Anthropic isn't just building models. It's building enterprise AI's connective tissue. Through standards, partnerships, and embedded defaults, Claude is becoming the platform. 💡

When Claude powers your CRM and your inbox, it's no longer a tool. It's the operating system.

Anthropic's $71 billion compute commitment makes this a very long game.

For brands and marketing leaders:

Your AI strategy is being decided by your vendors. The tools your team uses daily are selecting default models — and that shapes every output. This deserves a deliberate decision, not a default.

Who's making your team's AI stack decisions — you or your software vendors?
