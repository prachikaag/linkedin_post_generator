The three most powerful forces in AI just agreed they need a referee. 🤝

OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind are coordinating on shared safety standards. They've been in talks since July. That's significant — and largely unreported until recently.

The scope is real. Independent testing, cybersecurity protections, and incident reporting are on the table. So are potential coordinated limits on frontier-model development. (TechCrunch, Yahoo Finance)

Sam Altman committed to embedding third-party evaluators at OpenAI, mirroring Anthropic's existing practice. (Bloomberg) Anthropic's Dario Amodei called for coordination to avoid catastrophic risks. (Claims Journal)

Meanwhile, OpenAI is raising $30 billion at a $1.4 trillion valuation. The funding race and the safety push are running in parallel.

This isn't just a policy story. It's a structural shift. ⚡

When competing companies ask for oversight, the "move fast" era is ending. These aren't vague pledges — they're operational commitments with embedded evaluators and reporting requirements.

Worth paying attention to.

For business leaders, this changes how you evaluate AI partners. Auditability and safety practices matter now — not just capability benchmarks.

Ask your vendors: who is watching them?

Will industry self-regulation hold? Or will governments step in first — and is that the better outcome?
