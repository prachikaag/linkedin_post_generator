Four frontier AI models launched in 72 hours. The September race is on.

This month, every major AI company shipped something new. Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.1 on September 1. Google followed with Gemini 3.8 Flash the next day. OpenAI announced Astra that same week.

Fable 5.1 scores 52.6% on Terminal-Bench-Science — more than double Fable 5's 24.7%. 📊 Anthropic also cut cache-read pricing by 75%, lowering agent-heavy workloads by up to 45%. Gemini 3.8 Flash arrived just three weeks after 3.7 Flash. OpenAI released GPT Image 2.5 Flare on September 8. DeepSeek released V4.1 Flash on September 10. Eleven new models from seven providers in one month.

The pricing moves are as significant as the capability gains. When cache reads drop by 75%, building AI agents becomes affordable for teams that couldn't justify it before. These companies aren't iterating cautiously. They're competing on performance, speed, and price — all at once.

Here's what I think brands are missing: the barrier to building AI workflows isn't capability anymore. It's deciding to start. 🤔 The models are capable enough. The real question is whether your team's workflows are ready to use them.

If you're building AI-assisted processes, now is the time to run real tests. The gap between "good enough" and "genuinely useful" is closing fast. ⚡
