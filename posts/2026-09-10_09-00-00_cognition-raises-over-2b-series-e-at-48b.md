An AI coding agent just hit a $48 billion valuation. Let that sink in.

Cognition AI closed a $2 billion Series E this week. Their autonomous software agent, Devin, is now running inside NVIDIA, GE Aerospace, Citi, and Mercedes-Benz. Not in pilots. In production.

Revenue tells the real story: $492M run rate in May 2026. Nearly $900M today. 🚀

Here's what the data shows:

Andreessen Horowitz and Accel led the round — joined by Founders Fund, General Catalyst, NVIDIA, and about 30 other names. (Unite.AI)

The valuation nearly doubled from $26B just four months earlier. (American Bazaar Online)

And it's not the only funding story this week — Gimlet Labs raised $300M for AI hardware on the same day. (Tech Startups)

The pattern here is worth pausing on.

Enterprise AI adoption doesn't move in press cycles. It moves one internal approval at a time. Then suddenly NVIDIA is a reference customer. The $48B valuation reflects where the money thinks this is going — not where it is today.

What's actually changing: AI agents are getting budget codes and team leads assigned to them. They're no longer R&D experiments.

For brands and marketing teams, this matters more than it looks. 💡

If software work becomes agentic, creative and strategic work follows next. The question isn't whether AI agents come for your workflows — it's whether you'll be the one deploying them or reacting to them.

Are AI agents showing up in your team's work yet — or is this still a headline in your feed?
