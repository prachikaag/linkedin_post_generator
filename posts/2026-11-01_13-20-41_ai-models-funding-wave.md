Model launches, open-weight releases, and a $1.5B chip round — all in one month.

Here's what actually happened:

Tenstorrent closed a $1.5B Series E, doubling its valuation to $6.2B.
Trace Capital and Innovation X led the round.
The company is scaling Galaxy servers to run open-weight models.

Gemini 4 Argon reached broad availability on October 13.
OpenAI launched GPT-6.1 Terra on October 26.
It targets computer use, visual reasoning, and coding — at $1 per 1M input tokens.

Meta's Muse Spark Open arrived on October 15.
816 billion parameters, 42 billion active per token.
Multimodal: text, image, video, and audio — in a single open-weight model.
DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 also released under the MIT license, weights publicly available.
