Nvidia just agreed to buy Hugging Face for $12.9 billion.
Open-source AI is no longer a side project — it just became infrastructure. 💰

This is Nvidia's largest acquisition ever — 3 million models, 18 million developers, and half a million datasets set to sit inside the world's most powerful AI hardware company.
In the same week, Cognition raised $2 billion at a $48 billion valuation for Devin, its AI coding agent.

The Nvidia deal breaks down as $11.9 billion in cash plus $1 billion in equity retention for staff.
Jensen Huang says Hugging Face stays an open platform — developers won't be required to use Nvidia compute to build or deploy on it.
Cognition's annualised revenue nearly doubled in four months: from $492 million to close to $900 million.
Devin is already deployed at Citibank (for 40,000 developers), Goldman Sachs, GE Aerospace, Mercedes-Benz, and the US Army and Navy.

These aren't experiments — these are production deployments at serious scale. 🧠
The money tells the real story here: Nvidia buying Hugging Face isn't about owning models — it's about controlling the pipeline where open-source AI gets built and deployed.
Cognition's growth rate suggests that AI agents writing code aren't the future. They're already enterprise infrastructure.

For brands, the window to experiment quietly is closing.
AI agents handling full professional workflows — research, code, content — are now embedded at Citi and the US Navy.
If your team is still treating AI as a productivity add-on, that's the gap to close before 2027.
