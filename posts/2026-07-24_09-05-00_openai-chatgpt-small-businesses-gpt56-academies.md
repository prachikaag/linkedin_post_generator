This week, AI jumped the enterprise fence — for good. 🌐

OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and xAI all made major moves this week.
New models, automation tools, and a healthcare milestone landed back-to-back.
Washington is also waking up — an AI safety regulator may be coming.

OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Small Businesses with GPT-5.6 — three variants: Sol, Terra, and Luna.
Sam Altman noted Sol is 54% more token-efficient at coding than prior models.

Google DeepMind released Gemini 3.6 Flash — cutting output token usage by up to 17%.
Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite comes in at just $0.30 per million tokens.

A Google Research study on SymptomAI — built on Gemini 2.0 Flash — is striking.
In a 13,917-patient randomized trial, it outperformed independent clinicians by 2.47x.

The pattern isn't just "more models" — it's mass-market pressure from every direction. 🔍
OpenAI targets SMBs. Google targets clinicians. xAI open-sourced its coding agent.
All in one week.

The White House is reviewing a FINRA-style AI safety regulator for frontier models.
That changes the compliance conversation for everyone building on these tools.

ChatGPT now drives 8–12% of referral traffic for major media outlets — rivaling search.
AI Mode Ads are live. Your search strategy needs a rethink right now.

Is your team tracking what share of referral traffic is now coming from AI?
