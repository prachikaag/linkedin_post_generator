Your buyers are already shopping through AI. Are you visible to them?

The shift isn't coming. It happened.

AI search tools — Perplexity, ChatGPT, Google AI Mode — are where high-intent buyers now start research.

Brands still optimizing for traditional SEO are largely invisible there. 🔍

Here's what the data shows:

AI-referred traffic converts at 8x the rate of social media. (Salesforce Commerce Cloud)

Salesforce recently made Shopper, Buyer, and Merchant Agents generally available. They now connect directly with ChatGPT and Google Search.

Retailers running their own shopper agents grew sales 59% faster last holiday season.

At Walmart, 73% of marketing investment is now AI-enabled. Targeting, bidding, media, dynamic creative. (Chief Marketer) 📊

Google recently unified AI Overviews and AI Mode into one experience. AI now generates primary results for a growing share of queries.

The pattern is clear.

Discovery has moved. Social was the last big shift in where buyers started their journey. AI search is the next one.

The brands winning aren't replacing people. They're embedding AI into structured workflows. That's the Walmart model.

AI improves existing expertise. It doesn't replace it. 💡

So what does this mean Monday morning?

Audit your AI search visibility. Restructure content for AI discovery. Explore commerce agent options.

The brands still in pilot mode are already compounding a gap. ⚠️

Is your brand showing up when AI tools recommend products in your category?
