Billions in, in a single week. AI's money isn't slowing — it's concentrating. 💰

Several major funding rounds closed this month. Fal, Shield AI, and OpenAI all raised significant capital. The common thread: massive bets on infrastructure.

Fal raised $1.3B at a $16B valuation — led by Bessemer, with NVIDIA in the round. CEO Burkay Gur was candid: by summer, fal was rate-limiting customers. Demand had broken ahead of their capacity.

Shield AI raised $2.5B to acquire radar maker Echodyne for $5.3B. Its valuation hit $28.8B — up from $12.7B in March alone.

OpenAI secured $11B at a $1.25T valuation. It originally sought $30B at $1.4T. Even the most-funded AI company negotiates with reality. 🔍

Here's what I think brands are missing...

This isn't about individual companies winning. It's about who builds the rails. Fal's raise signals AI inference is now capital-intensive — not an afterthought. Shield AI is bundling autonomous software with sensor hardware. OpenAI's lower-than-targeted round shows even frontier models face limits.

Across 2026, AI companies have captured 80%+ of global VC. When capital concentrates this hard, it shapes which capabilities get built first. That's not a market story — it's a product story.

Watch where infrastructure dollars flow. That's where real AI capabilities emerge. Inference platforms like fal are scaling fast. Your team's toolkit looks different by next year.
