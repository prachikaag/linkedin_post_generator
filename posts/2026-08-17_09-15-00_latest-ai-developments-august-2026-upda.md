The AI model race just compressed a year of change into one month. 🚀

Every major lab shipped something this August. OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft, Google, xAI — all within days of each other. This isn't a normal release cycle. It's a sprint.

Here's what landed:

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Work — an agent connecting to Slack, Gmail, Drive, and Salesforce. It handles multi-hour projects on a schedule, bundled into existing plans. Luna, their cost-tier model, dropped 80% to $0.20 per million input tokens.

Anthropic's Claude Max offers near-Fable-5 performance at half the cost. Claude added real-time DLP enforcement for Enterprise. Microsoft's MAI-Image-2.5-Pro is claimed 84% cheaper than GPT-Image-2. Claude Opus 5 tops the August frontier rankings.

Here's what I think brands are missing...

The race isn't about which model wins. AI is now multi-tool territory — different models dominate different tasks. And with prices collapsing across the board, running several simultaneously is suddenly affordable.

So what does this mean for your team? 👇

Build a model strategy, not a single subscription. Match your creative anchor, automation workhorse, and compliance specialist. The "one tool fits all" era just ended.
