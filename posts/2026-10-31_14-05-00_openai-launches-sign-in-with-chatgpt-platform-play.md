AI just stopped being a tool. It's becoming the platform.

OpenAI launched Sign in with ChatGPT this month. Third-party apps can now run on the tier a user already pays for. That's not a feature. It's an operating system move.

The investment data tells the same story. 💰

In October, AI startup funding exceeded $43 billion across 105 rounds. The biggest deals weren't in generalist chatbots. They were in infrastructure, legal AI, and defence.

Daytona raised $60 million at a $1 billion valuation. It builds cloud infrastructure for running AI agents at scale. Legora closed $550 million at a $5.55 billion valuation — AI built specifically for legal workflows. Shield AI raised $1.5 billion at a $12.7 billion valuation for autonomous defence AI.

The pattern is clear: picks-and-shovels plays are winning the biggest checks. 🏗️

Meanwhile, Anthropic expanded its AI for Science program on October 28. Free Claude credits for research foundations globally. Science access, not just enterprise access.

The infrastructure layer is being built out fast. 🔨

Teams building pipes, platforms, and vertical tools are capturing more value. More than the teams who simply use them. That gap is widening in 2026.

For brands: you are a customer of this infrastructure. What do you actually own in the AI stack you depend on?

What AI infrastructure decision does your team rely on — and was it chosen deliberately?
