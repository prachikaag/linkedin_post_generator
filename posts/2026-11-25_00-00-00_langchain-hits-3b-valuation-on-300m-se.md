AI agents are no longer a future bet.

They are the infrastructure enterprises are actively building on. 🏗️

LangChain recently raised $300M at a $3B valuation. Walmart opened its retail systems to AI agents today. Google brought computer use to Gemini on Mac this week.

LangSmith, LangChain's enterprise platform, serves over 7,000 organizations. That is not experiment territory — that is real adoption.

Walmart let AI agents browse, cart, and check out for shoppers. Amazon upgraded AWS Bedrock AgentCore with 4x compute capacity.

OpenAI recently launched GPT-6.2 Astra for complex professional tasks. 🤖 Microsoft released seven in-house MAI models at Ignite 2026. Both aim to cut costs for enterprise developers building agents.

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 with a 1M-token context window. Skills over MCP is in public beta — signaling Anthropic's enterprise agent push.

Here's what I think brands are missing.

Agentic AI enterprise deployment is becoming the new infrastructure race. This is not one company winning — every layer of the stack is moving.

Walmart opening its aisles to agents is not just a retail story. It signals AI agents are graduating from pilot to plumbing.

Brands that ignore agent infrastructure now will face a steeper climb later. 📌
