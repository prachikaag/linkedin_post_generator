The AI funding machine isn't slowing down — it's accelerating. 🚀

November 2026 has seen 371 startup rounds totaling $38.2B — dominated by AI companies. Agentic platforms are pulling in billions from enterprise investors.

Mercor recently raised $750M in a Series D at a $20B valuation. It doubled in value in just over a year. Nvidia, Salesforce, Benchmark, and General Catalyst all backed the round. The company is approaching $2B in annualized gross revenue.

OpenAI anchored the month with an $11B close. The scale of enterprise AI investment in November is striking.

This isn't just a funding story. It's a signal about what enterprises are willing to pay for.

Agentic AI companies are winning because enterprise buyers are ready. They trust AI to run full workflows — not just assist with tasks. That shift is what the money is chasing.

BCG research adds the brand angle. AI agents don't just find brands — they evaluate them. Being visible to AI is different from being credibly recommended by AI.

Gartner surveyed 402 CMOs. They expect AI-driven automation to rise from 16% to 36% of marketing work by 2028.

Brands without AI-ready credibility now will be invisible to agents. They're making purchasing decisions for your customers. Worth paying attention to. 🎯

Is your brand visible — or just present — in the AI-driven buyer journey?
