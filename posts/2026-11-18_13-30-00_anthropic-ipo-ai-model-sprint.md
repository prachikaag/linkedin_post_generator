Three top AI providers released flagship models in the same week. ⚡

Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI all made major moves this week.

The Anthropic IPO roadshow has started — and the timing is no accident.

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 on November 9. It reclaimed the #1 LLM benchmark spot. Outputs are 30% faster and pricing dropped roughly 20%.

Anthropic also filed its S-1, targeting a $1.2–1.5 trillion valuation.

Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen on November 12. It runs 2.5x faster in high mode. Fabricated claims fell more than 5x versus Gemini 3.8 Flash.

OpenAI announced GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol on November 17. That brings November's total to 16 new models from 9 providers.

Here's the pattern: AI is getting cheaper and better faster than anyone expected. 🔄

When one provider improves, the others respond within days. That's new.

For brands and marketing teams, two things matter here. 💡

First, the tools you're already paying for are quietly getting better and cheaper.
