$9.8 billion invested in AI in a single week. 💰

Two stories are defining the AI space right now.

Capital is flooding into infrastructure. And agents are quietly moving into production.

In early November, 25 rounds totaled $9.8B. [4][5] That is not a typo.

fal raised $1.6B for inference. Shield AI secured $2.5B. Nominal closed $240M at a $3B valuation. [1]

Earlier this year, OpenAI closed a record $122B round. Its valuation hit $852B. It now generates $2B every month. [2][3]

88% of AI startup funding flows to U.S. companies. [4] The concentration is striking.

Separately, 57% of organizations now report AI agents in production. [6] 🤖

Here's what I think brands are missing...

The funding is not hype. It is infrastructure being built at scale.

When inference companies raise $1.6B, more capacity follows. Faster. Cheaper.

The agent frameworks are maturing fast. LangGraph, CrewAI, and Google ADK 2.0 are the new stack. [6]

Brands treating AI as "wait and see" are already behind.

The infrastructure is real. The agents are live. The window to build capability is now.
