Creative AI just had one of its biggest weeks. 🎨

Midjourney, Runway, and ElevenLabs all released major updates at once. The model layer upgraded too — GPT-5.6 Luna, Gemini 3.7 Flash, Claude Opus 5 in Perplexity.

Runway launched Agent Skills — letting agents build ad campaigns end-to-end. It's also moving subscribers to a new Max plan on September 1. ElevenLabs released Music v2 and enabled audiobook publishing to Spotify, with 100% royalties to creators. 🎵

Ideogram 4.0 went open-access. AWS pushed Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Web Search to general availability. Cloudflare launched Kitesurf — a browser runtime for AI agents, using 3–7x less CPU than Chromium. AI agent startup funding hit about $1.8 billion in July–August. 🤖

These tools aren't just adding features — they're becoming platforms. Runway's Agent Skills turn an ad workflow into an automated pipeline. AWS and Cloudflare are quietly becoming the backbone AI agents run on. The real competition has moved from models to platforms.

Marketing teams: agent-driven ad creation is live now. With $1.8B flowing into agentic AI platforms, the vendor landscape is about to shift fast. Map your stack before someone maps it for you.
