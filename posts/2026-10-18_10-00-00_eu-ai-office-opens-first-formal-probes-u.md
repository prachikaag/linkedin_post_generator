Europe just moved from AI policy to AI enforcement. 🔍
The EU AI Office opened formal probes against OpenAI and Anthropic — the first investigations under the AI Act.

They're examining both companies for systemic risk compliance: red-teaming, incident reporting, and model evaluations.
This is not a warning letter. It's accountability.
The AI investment market? Still at full speed.

Anthropic reportedly targets a November IPO at $100B+ — while under formal investigation. [1]
Shield AI raised $1.5 billion. TypeSafe AI closed an $870 million Series A. Modal tripled its valuation to $15.75 billion. [2]
Meta released Muse Spark Open — 816 billion parameters, 1 million token context, runs locally on consumer hardware. [3]
OpenAI rolled out browser automation in its ChatGPT Chrome extension this same week. [4]

Regulation and capital are arriving at the same time. That's new territory.

Companies now need regulatory operations — the same way they built safety teams.
The EU AI Act won't stop the wave. It will separate organized builders from reactive ones.
The most interesting companies right now are doing compliance as product work, not legal overhead.

If your brand uses frontier AI tools, this probe affects you indirectly.
The Act shapes the compliance posture of every platform you're paying for.
Audit your AI stack for EU AI Act readiness now — before your vendors do it for you. 💡
