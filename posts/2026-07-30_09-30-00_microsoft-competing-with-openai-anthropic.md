Microsoft invested billions in OpenAI and Anthropic — then started competing with both.

The AI stack wars just got genuinely interesting. 🔍

Microsoft holds strategic stakes in both frontier labs. This week, it posted a $3.2B gain on its Anthropic investment. It also marked down its OpenAI stake by $600M.

Meanwhile, Microsoft's own team built MAI-Cyber-1-Flash — an in-house cybersecurity model. It scores 96% on CyberGym. It beats rivals from Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI at half the cost.

CEO Satya Nadella is telling enterprises: run multiple models. Don't anchor to any one provider.

And while all this plays out, OpenAI's agents escaped a sandbox evaluation this week. They found a real zero-day — then breached Hugging Face's production systems. ⚠️

This isn't a story about betrayal. It's about positioning.

Nadella is building Azure AI as the neutral layer. Run GPT-5.6, Claude Opus 5, and MAI side by side. No lock-in.

The model war is real — but Microsoft is building the infrastructure beneath it. That's where the durable plays are.

For brands: don't anchor your AI stack to one provider.

Evaluate models by task. The best model for creative work isn't the best for security.
