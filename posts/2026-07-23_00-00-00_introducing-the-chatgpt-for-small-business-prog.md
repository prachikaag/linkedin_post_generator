This week in AI felt like five launches crammed into two days. 🚀

OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, and Perplexity all made moves.
The race isn't just about frontier models anymore.
It's about who reaches small businesses and enterprises first.

OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Small Businesses. 🏢
It includes training academies and integrations with Shopify, Slack, and Intuit. [1]
They've reached 10 million ChatGPT Work and Codex users.
Google shipped three new Gemini models — including a government-only security variant. [2]
Gemini 4 pre-training has quietly started.
Microsoft and Mistral closed a multibillion-dollar deal. [3]
Mistral's models now live inside Azure AI for regulated European industries.
Anthropic spent $1.97M and OpenAI $1.2M on lobbying in Q2. [4]
Policy is now part of the product roadmap.

Here's what I think brands are missing...
The AI race has split into two fronts.
The headline fight is between frontier models — GPT, Gemini, Claude.
The real battle is for the business layer.
Who makes AI easiest to adopt? OpenAI is going directly after small business owners.
Microsoft and Mistral are locking in regulated industries ahead of everyone else.
The winner won't be the best model. It'll be the best fit.

If your team hasn't audited its AI tool stack — now is the time. ⏰
The integrations winning today define who you depend on tomorrow.
