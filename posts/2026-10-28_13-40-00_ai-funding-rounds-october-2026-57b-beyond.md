$74.1 billion. 77 funding rounds. And October isn't over yet. 💰

That's the AI investment tally through this month alone. OpenAI's $20B mega-round gets the headline. But the $5.7B flowing to other AI companies is the story brands should actually be watching.

TypeSafe AI raised $870M at a $7.5B valuation. Instinct closed a $1B Series C. Mistral AI secured a $3.5B Series D led by Samsung. These aren't bets on one company winning — they're bets that the infrastructure layer of AI is still being built. 🏗️

Alongside the capital, product velocity is accelerating. GPT-6 Astra launched for Pro subscribers this month. Google has seven unreleased Gemini models in its pipeline. Every team racing for capital is also racing to ship.

The real question is what this means for marketing and brand teams picking AI tools today.

Here's what I think brands are missing...

When a company raises $1B, it's not just validation — it's a signal that they're building something with real switching costs. The AI tools your team adopts now may look very different in 12 months. ⚠️

I'm not saying don't commit. I'm saying build for change. Adopt tools that export your data. Document your prompts. Keep your institutional knowledge outside the platform.

The companies getting funded aren't slowing down. Neither should your thinking about how you use them.

Which AI tools has your team committed to in 2026 — and are you portable if they change?
