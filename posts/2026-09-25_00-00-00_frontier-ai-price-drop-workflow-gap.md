The cost of reaching frontier AI just collapsed.
The gap it leaves behind is organizational, not technical.

Four major models dropped this week.
GPT-6 Luna and Sol from OpenAI. Claude Opus 5.5 from Anthropic. Grok 4.7 from xAI.
All within 48 hours. September 2026 has seen 20 new AI models from 12 providers. The pace is unprecedented. 🚀

GPT-6 Luna lands at $0.10 input per million tokens — half its predecessor's price.
GPT-6 Sol and Grok 4.7 each come in at $2 input.
Claude Opus 5.5 is Anthropic's enterprise daily driver, priced at $4 input.
Both GPT-6 models carry a 1,050,000-token context window — a significant upgrade.

Here's what I think brands are missing...

The AI frontier model race is now producing a new problem: decision paralysis.
It's not access — teams have plenty of options.
Most have people experimenting ad hoc. Few have repeatable workflows that scale.

The teams winning are building agentic enterprise deployment workflows. 💡
Not better subscriptions — documented, repeatable processes any team member can follow.

Don't upgrade your model. Audit your workflow first.
Count how many team members use AI ad hoc versus following a documented process.
That gap is the real competitive risk for marketing teams.

What's your team's ratio of ad hoc AI use to structured workflow? I'd love to hear it.
