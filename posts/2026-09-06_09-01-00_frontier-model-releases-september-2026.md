Three frontier model launches dropped this week. All at once.

This isn't coincidence. Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google are racing — and pricing is falling with every round.

Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5.1 this week. Cache reads are now 75% cheaper than before. Agentic workloads could cost ~45% less. That's a real number for teams running automation at scale. 🔢

OpenAI released GPT-5.5 Thinking Pro — with distinct Thinking and Pro variants for harder, higher-accuracy work. They also launched GPT-5.6 Sol and Luna as new free-tier defaults. Unlimited text chats for free users too.

Google pushed Gemini Omni video generation to enterprise APIs. Omni Flash generates 720p video at $0.10 per second. Omni 1.1 Flash bumped that to 4K and 1080p — with character consistency across scenes. 🎬

Recently, Claude's voice mode started tapping into Gmail, Slack, Canva, and Notion directly.

Here's the pattern I keep seeing: the frontier is fragmenting.

Each lab is shipping specialised variants — voice, video, coding, reasoning — at dramatically lower prices. This isn't one model to rule them all. It's a portfolio era.

Brands treating AI as a single-point solution will get left behind.

Match capability to task. Different models for different workflows. Price sensitivity is now a real strategic lever.
