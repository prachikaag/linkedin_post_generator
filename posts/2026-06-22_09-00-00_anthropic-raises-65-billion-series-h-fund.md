$65 billion. That's not a typo. 🤑

Anthropic just closed a $65B Series H — one of the largest funding rounds in tech history. [1]

Meanwhile, Microsoft announced MAI-Thinking-1, its first in-house reasoning model. [2] And xAI is exploring a joint IPO with SpaceX at an $80B valuation. [3]

This isn't isolated. It's a pattern.

AI infrastructure investment hit a record $40B in the first half of 2026 alone — 3x the same period last year. [4] That's capital flooding into chips, data centers, and agentic platforms.

Smaller bets are flowing too. Odyssey raised $50M for world models that simulate physical environments from video. [5]

But Wired flags the real tension: valuations may be outpacing revenue multiples seen in previous tech cycles. [6]

Here's what I think brands are missing — this isn't just a funding story.

The infrastructure being built right now sets the ceiling for what AI can do in 2027 and beyond. Microsoft embedding MAI-Thinking-1 into Copilot means reasoning AI hits your team's daily workflow soon.

The real question is: are you watching from the sidelines, or building literacy now?

**For brand leaders:** Start mapping which workflows benefit from reasoning-first AI. The tools are coming whether you're ready or not.

What's your read — bubble, or a genuine infrastructure shift?
