Four model releases. One week. October is moving fast. 🏃

This isn't one company winning. It's the whole field sprinting in parallel.

Claude Haiku 5.5 launched on October 7. On a computer-use benchmark, it scored 72.4%.

Its predecessor scored 15.7%. That's not iteration. That's a different kind of model.

Mistral Large 4 entered public preview on October 6. One trillion parameters. Multimodal. Open-weight. Runs on four GPUs.

Reflection AI launched Beam on October 5. A frontier open-weight model built to rival leading Chinese models. At lower compute cost.

And OpenAI rolled out GPT-6 to all ChatGPT users this week. 🤖

Here's what I keep thinking about for brands...

The computer-use jump matters more than most teams realise right now.

When AI navigates real software at 72% accuracy — not a demo, a live benchmark — the conversation shifts. From "AI for content" to "AI for operations."

Competitor monitoring. Brief formatting. Content QA. Research. These don't need a coding team to automate any more.

And open-weight models are removing the cost barrier. Self-host a trillion-parameter model. No per-token fees at scale.

Gartner forecasts that 60% of brands will use agentic AI by 2028. I'd revisit that timeline. The tools are already here. ✅

What's one workflow your team hasn't tried automating yet?
