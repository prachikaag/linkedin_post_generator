$38.2 billion raised by AI companies. One month.

OpenAI secured $11 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation — with UAE sovereign funds among the backers. Fal.ai raised $1.3 billion at $16 billion. Mercor closed a $750 million Series D at $20 billion. DeepSeek reportedly closed a ~$15 billion round. The AI funding surge isn't slowing — it's widening.

One tracker counts 179 of November's 371 startup funding rounds going to AI companies — 48% of all capital deployed this month. Fal.ai's round was led by Bessemer Venture Partners, announced November 3. Mercor's $20 billion valuation makes it one of the most valuable HR-tech companies ever — built on AI hiring infrastructure. UAE sovereign wealth is now inside the company that builds the most-used AI tool on earth. 👀

What I find revealing is where the money flows. Not just foundation model builders. Infrastructure plays, talent tools, and Sovereign AI Fund initiatives from governments. This tells me the race isn't just between companies — it's between nations.

Here's what I think brands are missing: the tools you rely on are being shaped by interests very different from two years ago. Knowing who backs your AI stack isn't paranoia — it's due diligence. Worth paying attention to.

A practical starting point: map your AI vendors to their primary investors. The AI agent startup funding surge is real — but so is the geopolitical layer underneath it. 🌐
