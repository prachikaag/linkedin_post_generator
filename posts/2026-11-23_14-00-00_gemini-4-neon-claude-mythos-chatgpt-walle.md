Three new AI models. Two weeks. One clear signal for every brand team.

Google launched Gemini 4 Carbon on November 10 and Gemini 4 Neon on November 18. Anthropic released Claude Mythos 5.2 on November 9 — a price drop from $10 to $8 per million input tokens. OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet, letting its agents make purchases through Amex, Mastercard, and Visa.

The Gemini 4 family now spans Argon, Carbon, and Neon — each tuned for different price-performance profiles. Claude Mythos 5.2 came in cheaper than its predecessor while maintaining benchmarks. ChatGPT Wallet initially landed for Pro subscribers in the US. OpenAI also launched a Business Hub where companies can claim profiles — more search directory than chat tool. 📲

The pattern isn't about which model wins. It's about compression — better performance, lower costs, more commerce capability arriving faster than brands can absorb. Claude Mythos 5.2 and Gemini 4 Neon launching within days of each other isn't random. The model upgrade cycle has become a marketing event in its own right.

Here's what I think brands are missing: when ChatGPT Wallet becomes standard, the question isn't just "can AI help us create content" — it's "can AI complete purchases on behalf of our customers?" That is a fundamentally different commerce challenge.

Build a "model monitoring" habit the same way you monitor competitors. A monthly 30-minute review. The teams doing this aren't just keeping up — they're spotting leverage before others do. 💡
