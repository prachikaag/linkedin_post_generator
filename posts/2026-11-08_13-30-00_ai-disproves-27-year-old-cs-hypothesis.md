AI just disproved a 27-year-old computer science theory. No human involved.

Both OpenAI and Anthropic recently announced a stunning result. 🤯 Their models independently solved the Exponential Time Hypothesis — a foundational conjecture from 1999. Scientists reacted with shock, vindication, and even regret. [3]

Anthropic's paper credits Claude with finding the core algorithm. [1] It used 14 million output tokens — with zero human input. [2] OpenAI made the same announcement hours later. The formal proof hasn't been independently verified yet. Worth noting.

This isn't just a research win. It signals what agentic AI systems can do at full autonomy. The funding surge around agentic AI makes more sense now. Anthropic also warned this week it's nearing recursive self-improvement capability — where AI trains better AI. [4] That's a significant moment.
