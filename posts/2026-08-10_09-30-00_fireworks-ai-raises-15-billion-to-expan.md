Three AI funding announcements in July totaled over $52 billion in committed capital.

That number deserves a moment.

This is no longer about technology bets. The money is building the infrastructure of the next decade. 💰

MGX's $49 billion Fund I backs Anthropic, OpenAI, and xAI. It is also co-developing what could be Europe's largest AI campus near Paris.

Fireworks AI closed a $1.5 billion Series D at a $17.5 billion valuation. It now serves over 40 trillion tokens daily — nearly triple its earlier volume. Annual revenue has surpassed $1 billion.

Travis Kalanick's startup Atoms raised $1.7 billion, led by a16z. The focus: food, mining, and transportation automation — physical AI at industrial scale.

The funding converges on two themes. 💡

First: inference infrastructure. Fireworks AI and Together AI ($800M) are scaling the inference layer enterprise AI runs through.

Second: physical AI. Atoms represents a wave of startups moving AI from software into the real world.

Gartner forecast 40% of enterprise apps will embed AI agents by year-end. That's up from less than 5% in 2025. The capital is following the deployment wave.
