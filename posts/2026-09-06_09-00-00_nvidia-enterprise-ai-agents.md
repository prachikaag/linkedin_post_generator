Enterprise AI just crossed a threshold this week. 🤖

Nvidia, Anthropic, Google, and Writer all made major moves. The common thread? AI agents that act without being asked.

Nvidia's Agent Toolkit launched at GTC 2026 with 17 enterprise partners. Adobe, Salesforce, and SAP are among them. It's the first major platform to launch with built-in security. Analysts still flag governance gaps across its five-vendor framework.

Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 dropped this week — built cheaper for coding and multi-step agent workflows. Claude Fable 5.1 followed. Cache reads got 75% cheaper. Agentic workload costs drop by up to ~45%, at $10/M input tokens.

Writer went further. Their agents trigger themselves — no prompt needed. They detect signals across Gmail, Slack, Gong, and Google Calendar, then execute workflows automatically.

Google's Gemini Omni Flash added video to the mix. It generates 720p content at $0.10/second — from image, audio, and text inputs combined. 🎬

The pattern is clear to me. Agentic AI is moving from "ask and receive" to "watch and act." That changes how enterprise teams design workflows from scratch. The agent isn't waiting for instructions anymore.

For brands and marketing teams, this isn't theoretical anymore. The platforms your stack runs on are being rewired around autonomous agents. The question isn't whether to prepare — it's how fast.
