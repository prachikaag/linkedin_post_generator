The AI industry is rewriting records and org charts — in the same week. 🚀

OpenAI is raising $15B at a $1.25 trillion valuation. Anthropic's IPO is six times oversubscribed. And Nvidia just lost the CEO who led it for 33 years.

Capital and leadership are both in motion — at the same time.

OpenAI's round — still closing — targets a $1.25 trillion valuation, per Benzinga. That ranks it among the world's most valuable companies.

Anthropic's IPO is tracking six times oversubscribed, days before listing (Bloomberg). Public markets are clearly ready to bet on AI.

Jensen Huang resigned from Nvidia after 33 years. CFO Colette Kress takes over as interim CEO (NVIDIA Newsroom). Shares fell over 4% — with earnings just days away (Al Jazeera).

Agile Robots raised $500M led by SoftBank at an $8.5B valuation (SiliconANGLE). Revenue agent startup Rox closed a $100M Series B at $1.3B (Venture Daily Digest).

Three things are converging: AI IPOs, chip leadership flux, and robotics funding.

Anthropic's six-times-oversubscribed IPO shows public investors are ready for AI companies.

OpenAI's $1.25 trillion valuation isn't hype — it's the market treating AI as core infrastructure.

The companies building your AI tools are now entering public markets. That shifts incentives, pricing power, and what they build next.

Watch the Nvidia transition too. Chip supply shapes everything downstream. 💡
