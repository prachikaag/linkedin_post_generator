AI assistants just became AI coworkers. That line matters more than it sounds.

Perplexity launched "Teammates" this week — persistent AI agents that handle ongoing background tasks while you do other things. These aren't smarter chatbots. 🤖 They run in parallel, investigate independently, and keep going when you close the app.

The same week, Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and added a Personal Finance connector. Connect up to 16 banks. 24 months of transaction history. All read-only, in US beta.

Google shipped Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash — a conversational video model, available now for $0.07 per second at 720p. 🎬

None of these is an incremental update. They're different kinds of AI relationships.

What catches my attention isn't the feature — it's how Perplexity named it. Not a "smarter search." A Teammate. That framing signals where user expectations are heading fast.

Here's what I think brands are missing: the question is no longer "which AI tool do we subscribe to?" It's "what do we trust our AI colleagues to handle unsupervised?" Those are very different risk and governance conversations.

Marketing and brand teams: now is the time to map out what tasks you're genuinely comfortable delegating. Not every workflow. Not yet. But start with the ones where AI makes no consequential decisions alone.

What's one task you've genuinely handed off to AI completely — and what made you comfortable doing it?
