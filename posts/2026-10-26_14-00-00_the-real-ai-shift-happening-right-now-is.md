AI stopped answering questions. Now it's taking action. 🤖

Something fundamental shifted in October 2026.
Every major lab — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft — shipped autonomous agents.
This isn't an upgrade. It's a category change.

OpenAI built "dots" — background agents that run continuously while you sleep.
Anthropic's Claude now anticipates needs before you ask.
Microsoft's Copilot gained Autopilot mode — multi-step actions across Office, no hand-holding needed.
Google's Gemini 4 Argon launched for enterprise with multi-agent orchestration built in. ⚡

Here's what I think brands are missing...
The "prompt and wait" era is ending.
The next era is AI as a digital coworker. One that acts, follows through, and adapts.
That shifts what you hire for, how you build workflows, and the oversight you need.

Start experimenting with agentic workflows now — not next quarter.
Map two or three repetitive brand tasks that run on clear rules.
Those are your first pilot candidates.
