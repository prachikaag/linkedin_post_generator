$1.81 billion. One day. 46 AI companies. 💰

October 8, 2026 set a record for AI funding in a single session. That's not a trend line anymore — it's a vertical.

The rounds aren't random. They're getting more specialised by the week.

Manus, the Chinese AI lab, raised over $500 million. It's the company's first round since Meta's acquisition fell through. Investors include Boyu Capital, IDG Capital, and Tencent.

Shield AI raised a $1.5 billion Series G at a $12.7 billion valuation. Legora closed a $550 million Series D. Arena raised $200 million in a Series B from Lightspeed and Khosla Ventures.

These aren't early bets anymore. 📊

This is institutional capital moving into mature AI applications. The categories getting funded — agentic security, legal AI, workflow automation — are production software. Not blue-sky research.

For brand leaders, this matters. 🔎

The tools shaping your workflows in 2027 are being funded right now. Watching where capital goes tells you where capability will land.

A well-funded AI tool is more likely to still be here in 18 months. That matters for any team building AI into their workflow.
