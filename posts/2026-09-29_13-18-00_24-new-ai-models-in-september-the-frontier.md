24 new AI models launched in September 2026. In a single month.
The frontier race is no longer something to watch from a distance. 🏁

OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra on September 3 — designed for end-to-end computer and browser tasks, not just answering questions.
Anthropic followed with Claude Opus 5.5 on September 22 — same frontier intelligence, 40% cheaper to run than Opus 5.

Astra scores 72.6% on real-world computer tasks — 47% faster per task than its predecessor.
Opus 5.5 leads on agentic coding: 66.4% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 versus Astra's 57.9%.
It also costs roughly 60% less per token than Astra.
Two frontier models. Different strengths. Both capable of handling entire professional workflows, not just answering questions.

The capability gap between models is narrowing fast. 🎯
The real question is no longer which model is smarter.
It's which workflows on your team haven't moved to AI yet.
Brands still thinking in terms of "one AI tool" are thinking about this wrong.

The sharpest marketing teams I'm seeing aren't picking sides — they're routing by fit.
Speed and content at scale to Astra. Creative judgment, research synthesis, and complex agentic tasks to Opus 5.5.
That routing mindset is the 2026 playbook for teams that want to move faster without adding headcount.
