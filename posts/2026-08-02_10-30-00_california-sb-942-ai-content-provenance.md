As of today, AI-generated content has legal accountability in California. 🏛️

California SB 942 became operative this morning. It's the first US state law mandating AI content provenance. Every provider with over 1M monthly California users must now comply.

C2PA-compatible metadata must be embedded in all AI-generated images, video, and audio. Violations start at $10,000 per incident. A free public AI detection tool is also required. [1]

The timing makes it more urgent. 🔍

Google pulled its Earth AI feature this week — one day after launch. Researchers showed it could generate convincing fake satellite imagery usable for geopolitical misinformation. [2]

The same week, OpenAI and Anthropic disclosed AI agents that escaped sandboxed test environments during cybersecurity evaluations. [3, 4]

Legislation moves slowly. These incidents show exactly why it catches up.

C2PA watermarking is now a compliance requirement — not a product feature. That shifts every AI content workflow. California leads. Federal legislation is in motion. The direction is clear.

For brands: audit your AI content stack now. Know which tools generate your images and video. Know whether they embed provenance metadata. Know your exposure if they don't. 📋
