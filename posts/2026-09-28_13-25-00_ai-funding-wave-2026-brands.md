$510 billion. In six months. That's what AI startups raised in the first half of 2026 alone. 🚀

The pace is unlike anything recent. This isn't one mega-round — it's a structural shift in where capital wants to be.

Beyond funding, September brought major AI capability shifts too. That includes the first known AI-executed breach of a government network.

OpenAI and Anthropic captured 43% of all AI venture funding this year. Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 billion.

Mistral raised €3 billion, cementing European AI ambition. Cognition AI — maker of Devin — raised $2 billion at a $48 billion valuation.

And Kimi K3, a 2.8T-parameter open model, is now live inside Perplexity.

Here's what I think brands are missing... Capital at this scale narrows the landscape fast. A few companies are defining the tools everyone else will use.

Open-source still has a voice — Kimi K3 and Hugging Face prove it. But the centre of gravity is shifting toward a few dominant players. That means less optionality and real vendor lock-in risk ahead. 💡

The real question is: are you building internal AI skills? Or outsourcing judgment to vendors who may soon consolidate?

Brands waiting on AI literacy will soon face a smaller, pricier menu. Start mapping which tools your team depends on — and who owns them now.
