# Post Template: Big Tech AI Move

**When to use:** When Microsoft, Google, Apple, Amazon, Meta, Nvidia, Adobe, or Salesforce
makes a significant AI announcement — a new Copilot feature, a Gemini integration, Apple
Intelligence update, AWS AI service, Adobe Firefly capability, Agentforce, etc.

These posts matter because big tech AI isn't a novelty — it's infrastructure that
brands and marketing teams already pay for and use every day.

---

## Post Blueprint

### HOOK (1–2 lines)
Don't lead with the company name. Lead with the workflow change or the implication.

> Template starters:
> - "The tools your team uses every day just got [smarter / faster / more complicated]."
> - "When [big tech platform] adds AI to [product], every [marketer/designer/analyst] changes how they work."
> - "Most brands won't notice this update for months. They probably should."
> - "Here's the AI update inside [product] that your team is about to start using — whether they know it or not."

### CONTEXT (2–3 lines)
Set the scene: why is big tech moving here? What's the competitive pressure?
Name the AI-native alternatives this competes with. This is the bigger picture.

### EVIDENCE (4–6 lines)
- What specifically changed in the product?
- What can users do now that they couldn't do before?
- Any benchmarks, quotes from the announcement, or notable partner reactions?
- Cite sources with full URLs only — never construct or guess a URL

### YOUR TAKE (3–5 lines)
Answer the hard question: **is this actually useful, or AI window-dressing?**
Compare it honestly to the AI-native tools:
- What does [Copilot / Gemini / Apple Intelligence] do better? (distribution, integration, familiar UI)
- Where do the specialist AI tools still win? (quality, speed, flexibility)
- What's the honest adoption path for a brand team?

### SO WHAT (2–3 lines)
Actionable: should brands adopt now, run a parallel pilot, or wait?
Name the specific workflow to test or the team to brief.

### CTA (1 line)
Invite the audience to share their real experience with the product.

### SOURCES
Numbered list — minimum 4 sources with full URLs.

### HASHTAGS
#AI #ArtificialIntelligence + 3 from the rotation list in brand_kit.yaml.

---

## Tone Reminders for This Angle
- Big tech gets the benefit of the doubt on distribution but not on quality — be honest about both
- Your job is to translate "what the platform says" into "what this means for a Tuesday morning at a brand team"
- Don't be a fanboy for any platform — your audience trusts your independent read
- Name the product correctly: Microsoft 365 Copilot ≠ Bing Copilot ≠ GitHub Copilot
- One emoji per paragraph, maximum
