# Post Template: AI Feature or Product Launch

**When to use:** Any time an AI company ships a new model, feature, API update, or drops
a significant YouTube demo/explainer. This covers: ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini,
ElevenLabs, Midjourney, Runway, Sora, Mistral — any AI tool launch.

---

## Post Blueprint

### HOOK (1–2 lines)
Lead with the concrete capability shift — not "Company X announced Y" but what changed.

> Template starters to adapt:
> - "You can now [do thing that wasn't possible before]."
> - "In the time it takes to make a coffee, [AI tool] can now [specific task]."
> - "The gap between what AI can do and what most brands are using it for just got wider."

### CONTEXT (2–3 lines)
Set the broader landscape. This release doesn't exist in a vacuum — what's the trend
it's part of? Name 1–2 other companies or recent moves that sit alongside it.

### EVIDENCE (4–6 lines)
Cite the specific features, benchmarks, or quotes from the launch. Weave in 2–3 sources.
Use the exact URLs from the news articles — never construct or guess a URL.

> If the company dropped a YouTube video demonstrating the feature, name it:
> "In their demo video, [Company] showed [specific thing] — and it's worth watching."

### YOUR TAKE (3–5 lines)
Answer the question: **what can brands actually do with this that they couldn't before?**
Be specific. Give a concrete use case — content creation, campaign ideation, customer service,
personalisation, production speed. Name the workflow that changes.

### SO WHAT (2–3 lines)
One or two actionable things a brand team or marketing leader should do this week:
- Test it on [specific workflow]
- Compare it against [existing tool] on [specific task]
- Brief your creative team on [specific implication]

### CTA (1 line)
Ask a question that invites the audience to share their own experience.

### SOURCES
Numbered list — minimum 4 sources with full URLs.

### HASHTAGS
#AI #ArtificialIntelligence + 3 from the rotation list in brand_kit.yaml.

---

## Tone Reminders for This Angle
- Be excited but not breathless — lead with *impact*, not wow
- Acknowledge the catch: cost, limitations, what it doesn't do well yet
- Compare to alternatives honestly (this is where your credibility comes from)
- One emoji per paragraph, maximum

---
