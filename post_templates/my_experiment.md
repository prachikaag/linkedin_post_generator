# Post Template: Human-in-the-Loop Experiment

**When to use:** When you've personally tested an AI tool, workflow, or approach and want
to share honest results. These are first-person posts — not driven by news, driven by
your own experience. They're your most credible content.

**How to trigger:** Log your experiment in `experiments/ideas_log.md` and run:
```
Run the LinkedIn Post Generator pipeline with --angle experiment
```

---

## Post Blueprint

These posts follow a slightly different structure to the news-driven posts.

### HOOK (1–2 lines)
The surprising result OR the question that made you run the experiment.
Lead with the twist, not the setup.

> Template starters:
> - "I spent [X time] testing [AI tool] on [specific task]. The result surprised me."
> - "Most people use [AI tool] for [obvious thing]. I tried it for [unexpected thing]."
> - "Here's what actually happens when you run [specific workflow] through [AI tool]."
> - "The honest answer to 'is [AI tool] worth it for [use case]' — after I actually tested it."

### THE EXPERIMENT (2–3 lines)
What you set out to do. Be specific:
- What tool? (name + version if relevant)
- What task? (e.g. "writing a brand positioning brief for a new client")
- What was your measure of success?

### WHAT WORKED (3–4 lines)
The concrete wins. Use numbers where real:
- Time saved ("2 hours → 25 minutes")
- Quality uplift ("the hook it wrote was better than my first draft")
- Workflow change ("I now use it for X step, not Y step")

### WHAT DIDN'T (2–3 lines)
The honest friction. This is what makes people trust you:
- What did the AI get wrong, miss, or struggle with?
- Where did you have to step in and fix it?
- What would you NOT use it for after this?

### YOUR TAKE (3–4 lines)
The synthesis: what does this experiment tell you about AI's current state for brands?
Connect it to a broader pattern if relevant.

### SO WHAT (2 lines)
One concrete recommendation for someone who wants to run a similar experiment.

### CTA (1 line)
Invite the audience to share their own experiments.

### SOURCES (if any)
If you referenced any tools, articles, or research — list them.
If it's purely your own experiment, skip this section.

### HASHTAGS
#AI #ArtificialIntelligence + 3 from the rotation list in brand_kit.yaml.

---

## Tone Reminders for This Angle
- First person throughout — this is YOUR experiment, YOUR observations
- Honest > polished. Admitting failure builds more credibility than hiding it
- Specific > general. "Claude gave me 3 usable hooks out of 10" beats "the results were mixed"
- Avoid "game-changer" — describe the actual change instead
- One emoji per paragraph, maximum

## Example Post Skeleton

```
[Surprising result or the question that made you do this]

[The experiment: tool + task + what you were trying to achieve]

[What worked: the specific, concrete wins]
[What worked cont: time saved, quality shift, workflow change]

[What didn't: honest friction and where you had to step in]

[Your take: what this tells you about AI for brands right now]
[Your take: the pattern you see beyond this one tool]

[So what: one recommendation for your audience]

[CTA question inviting them to share their own tests]

#AI #ArtificialIntelligence #AITools #FutureOfWork #MarketingAI
```

## The Human-in-the-Loop Framing
Every one of these posts reinforces your core positioning:
**You are the human editor. AI is your drafter.**

The posts show you directing, testing, and making judgment calls — not being replaced by AI,
not being naive about it either. That's the expertise your audience is here for.
