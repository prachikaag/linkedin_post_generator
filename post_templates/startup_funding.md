# Post Template: AI Startup Funding News

**When to use:** Any time an AI startup announces a funding round, acquisition,
IPO filing, or major valuation milestone. This is about following the money
to understand where AI is heading — and what it means for brands.

---

## Post Blueprint

### HOOK (1–2 lines)
Lead with the signal, not the number. The dollar amount is context; the *why* is the story.

> Template starters to adapt:
> - "When [amount] flows into [category], pay attention."
> - "Investors just made a loud bet on [specific capability] — and it tells us something."
> - "Not all AI funding rounds are created equal. This one matters."

### CONTEXT (2–3 lines)
Why now? Name the problem this company is solving and why capital is flowing to it at
this moment. If multiple companies are raising in the same space, zoom out to the pattern.

### EVIDENCE (4–6 lines)
- The raise: amount, round, lead investors, key details
- What the company does — be specific, not vague ("an AI platform")
- If available: customer numbers, growth stats, or notable clients
- Cite sources with full URLs only (from the article input — never construct a URL)

### YOUR TAKE (3–5 lines)
Answer: **what does this funding signal for brands?**
- Is this a tool category brands should be testing now?
- Does this displace an existing tool or workflow?
- Is this a category to watch but not touch yet?
- What does the investor backing tell you about the sector's direction?

### SO WHAT (2–3 lines)
Concrete action for the audience:
- "Add [company] to your watchlist"
- "If you're currently using [competitor], it's worth a pilot"
- "This is the category to get your team upskilled on before it's mainstream"

### CTA (1 line)
A question that invites people to share their own context.

### SOURCES
Numbered list — minimum 4 sources with full URLs.

### HASHTAGS
#AI #ArtificialIntelligence + 3 from the rotation list in brand_kit.yaml.

---

## Tone Reminders for This Angle
- Follow the money but don't be naive — funding ≠ product quality
- Be specific about what the company actually does (avoid "AI platform" or "AI-powered X")
- Zoom out to pattern when multiple raises are happening in the same space
- Your credibility is in the *interpretation*, not the announcement
