# AI Experiments Ideas Log

Log your AI experiments and test ideas here. Each entry becomes a LinkedIn post
when you run the pipeline with `--angle experiment`.

Keep entries honest and specific — the more concrete the notes, the better the post.

---

## How to Use

1. Add an experiment below using the template
2. Run: `Run the LinkedIn Post Generator pipeline with --angle experiment`
3. The post-generator reads the most recent "ready to post" entry and turns it into a draft

---

## Entry Template

```markdown
### [Experiment Title]
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** idea | in-progress | done | ready-to-post

**Tool(s) used:** e.g. Claude Sonnet 4.6, Midjourney v7, ChatGPT-4o
**Task:** What were you trying to do?
**Goal:** How did you define success?

**What worked:**
- (be specific — times, quality outcomes, surprises)

**What didn't:**
- (be honest — failures, limitations, workarounds needed)

**Time before:** How long did this take you without AI?
**Time after:**  How long did it take with AI?

**My take:** What does this tell you about AI for brands?
**Recommendation:** One concrete thing your audience should try or avoid.

**Notes for post:** Any specific quotes, screenshots, or data points to include?
```
